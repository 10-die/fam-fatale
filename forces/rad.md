# RAD

("RAD" — spelled out once as "r a d" in the source.)

Transcription note: voice-to-text often renders "rad" as "rat" and sometimes as "red." Both are read as **rad** here, as the 2026-10-10 raw headers also say.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md (header: "\"rat\" = **rad**"); raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1 ("red, r a d")

## Overview
- A species/faction distinct from humans, occupying their own territory ("red territory"). Not inherently hostile — Loading's own arc is realizing that non-sick RAD flee rather than fight him, and only sick RAD rush him regardless (see characters/loading.md).
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Have to fight to avoid dying/getting sick; see mechanics/damage-and-combat.md and mechanics/sick.md for the fight-to-survive vs. choose-to-be-sick split.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2
- Organized into families/units that fight together and become physically linked as a result — see mechanics/family-link.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2
- Starting arc 2, many/most RAD manifest actual powers as a "metamorphosis," earned through effort/training rather than arbitrary — see mechanics/damage-and-combat.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2
- Organized into at least two named territories, Gucci and McConnell, with different relationships to SICK. See forces/rad-territories.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- **Radiation boost and where power comes from.** "radiation boost for the strongest fighters. And power comes purely from dedication to constant evolution, learning, experimentation."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Arc 1 — humans' relationship to RAD territory
- Arc 1 opens with an investigation into pre-collapse tech (300–400 years old), which surfaces the Internet as global news. Humans, already struggling against fight-SICK, put it to a global vote (see forces/humans.md) and decide to send an extraction operation into RAD territory to recover as much old tech as strategically possible — trucks and planes extracting tech while fighting off "the horde of the RAD."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Separately (possibly the same operation, possibly a later one — not specified), a smaller embedded team — one RAD and one human, or per a second count in the same paragraph, two RAD and two humans plus Winter — goes deep into RAD territory to build a "sick immunisation fighting machine," surviving without going sick under Winter's direct instruction. See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md for the headcount discrepancy.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## Rad live to fight; humans fight to live
- "Their entire reason for existing is because they have to fight to live. Whereas the rad, they live to fight. They're having a blast. Even in the sick territory, they're having so much fun. It's ridiculous." 10die expects people to assume McConnell territory is "some dark, dreary, dreadful, corporal, mechanical, sludge fest of horrors. No."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Whereas the rad, they live to fight")
- **Sick rad vs. Deguchi rad, as values (2026-10-07).** "Sick represents fighting to kill. The Deguchi represent Fighting for the love of fighting. Fighting for the love of bonding, for understanding, for experimenting." And the trap that keeps the sides apart: "the humans don't want to risk being sick, and the rad don't want to lose sick. They are completely dependent all the time. And they are all at their peak all the time. All lethal. All killers. All sick." (That passage reads as describing sick rad generally; it sits beside the Deguchi line, not against it.)
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3

## How rad approach a fight
- **Like deer; the greeting is a fist.** A human greets another creature by sending energy toward it to be perceived; "Keegan does so with their fist. Rad will eventually try to hit you if they're interacting with you. They're like deer, right? But they only want one thing: to hit and get hit for a while."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("They're like deer, right?")
- **Healthy rad don't chase; they fight what's closest.** "Rad don't chase, or at least rad that aren't sick don't chase." Mary Sue fights them and escapes; "they'll follow for a bit, and they'll interact with another rad and start fighting. So it's because they choose to fight what's closer." Keegan's hyperfocus is the exception (characters/).
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Rad don't chase")
- **Coy vs. direct.** "Rad in general wants to fight. Some will be like coy about how they path towards you. Some won't be direct. Sick Rad and Keegan are direct. They walk right towards you and start a fight."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("Sick Rad and Keegan are direct")
- **Strong Deguchi are still distractible.** Other strong Deguchi rad hunt Mary Sue "very well, very sickly," but "because they are still Deguchi at heart, they are still distracted by the fight right in front of them... another rad will interact with them with a punch, and now they're in a new fight."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("very well, very sickly")

## The brawl contract
- "Now with regular Rad, when they eventually start fighting, when they're not sick, they'll fight, they'll fight, they'll fight. Then if you hit them back with a few blows, they become satisfied and they leave. Like they only wanted a quick brawl. Not like a title weight fight in several minutes, just a few seconds of back and forth, swinging. Mm, thank you."
- "These are the rad who are safe enough for humans to actually live in those territories. Because it's essentially a contract. The rad will fight you here. They will not go easy on you. But you hit them enough times, and they stop fighting and start almost bonding. Being affectionate."
- Two outcomes: "they'll fight you, and they'll leave. Or they'll fight you, and if it was a really interesting fight, they'll try to bond with you a little bit. If you don't want they'll leave."
- "Keegan, does not leave."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("it's essentially a contract")
- Why Deguchi can let humans in: "That's why the Deguchi territory starts letting humans in, because there isn't sick. If they were sick, the humans would get ripped apart."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("starts letting humans in")

## Culture: the Brawl as music
- Prompted by a kindergarten concert down the street: "Culture. is the personhood of a collective to agree to keep the whole neighborhood pumping with music because they're enjoying something together."
- "imagine the rad make music with their bodies while they fight and then together they create a cacophony of music and it's like every beat is like a piece of a different song and then it transitioned into a completely different style for a bit for a few bars."
- From a distance: "you'd hear a fight here and a fight there in the distance knowing that there's a large group of rad there brawling and if you join them you're gonna be part of the music."
- "they're brawling with love because they're not sick they're not trying to eliminate each other they're trying to beat each other up and go on and just try to fight someone else."
- It builds: "it reaches a crescendo where the music is, becomes very loud and then it attracts other smaller clusters and it, the energy ramps up." Unlike the kids' concert, "Imagine if it doesn't stop, it just keeps building and attracting more."
- Styles fight, then merge: "you'll see two different like vibes of songs like the South like Southern Twang having a fight with They're changing in music, changes together, and it starts to flow together. Intentionally." Fighters move from damage to understanding: "one time, you'll be fighting someone there randomly. The other time, you'll be fighting alongside them against a few others."
- Agency and drift: "you still have control but you don't have control over the change the change the I'm gonna do this, you know what, whoa, I'm dro- I'm going down, a level, oh, I'm fighting these people now, that was a good kick, I dodge it."
- "Mary Sue tends to live as far away from them as possible because you just have to interact with everything."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1
- **The dark version.** When the strongest sick Deguchi fight each other they set off "chain reactions. And a, a vicious, disgusting, revolting battle. Revoltingly alluring. Sound that feels like a force of nature... All of these rad singing while fighting. Singing, rapping, humming, whistling, la la lying along together." (forces/rad-territories.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2

## Rad economy cycles
- **Boom, bust, build.** "It's not the same rad each time. Every rad has moments of abject poverty behavior. No place. Then they like lock in and they get like build, they build things for themselves and build buildings and structures and contraptions to move them around the structures, to shoot them around their own structures." This follows directly on a list that ends "Hyperdiverse capitalistic bouts and then recessions," which may be a territory type or a description of this cycle (canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1
- **Deguchi's socioeconomic ladder.** "The weaker rad eventually get bored of being, like, just fighting, like, just casually like humans, and start becoming more aggressive, and then they'll fight their way to the top just as another rad goes down, just as another killer goes down. So now this one that was down at the lowest socioeconomic level of like gym fights as opposed to Colosseum marathons of war, so every rad eventually gets a chance to go to the top." McConnell has no bottom. (forces/rad-territories.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("In the Deguchi territory, there's a cycle")
