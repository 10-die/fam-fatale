# Human society

How humans govern themselves, own things, live, work, learn, and get around. Split out from forces/humans.md (which keeps the July government/voting-app entries, the border, temperament, and the human–rad relationship). King as a person lives in characters/king.md; this file only covers the institutions. Contradictions are logged in canon/_oq/humans.md.

## A government of everyone
- "The governments are everyone." 10die: "I would love some, anyone, any political theorist to wonder if it's possible to have a government of everyone. Everyone has a stake, an equal stake."
- How change happens: "if you have a good idea, people will go with you. If you don't have a good idea, they'll immediately join someone else or start their own idea. So it's through individuals having ideas that a large majority agree with. That's how things change. Like overwhelming majority."
- This is why the response to a sick outbreak is total: "Humanity just collapses all of its resources on it. That's what the governments do, because the governments are everyone." See mechanics/sick-response.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
- (The 2026-10-03 archive doc lists real-world political thinkers next to this idea: Athenian democracy, Hirschman, panarchy, liquid democracy, Bookchin, Kropotkin, Rousseau. That list is assistant research, not 10die's canon.)
  source: archive/sessions/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society/post-collapse-society.md

## Collective ownership
- "On Earth, what has changed is that the governments now include everyone. The kingdoms include everyone. Every human, every human or rad who is part of a collective. is part owner of that collective. So we no longer have egos of one, we have egos of collectives."
- 10die sets it against Westeros / King's Landing under the Lannisters: life "under the hand of dominators surrounded by other dominators," a king who says "I am not your king, I am my king. I do what I want." (Compare King's own July line, "I would not be my King I would be your king," in characters/king.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- Big business is folded into this: "Big business is the government. And, and everyone's a part of the government. And everyone agrees. No big business." See *Small business* below.
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- July has King holding "part ownership in everything and everyone," and American citizens who "all share their half of the ownership of their government." See characters/king.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶16
- Mary Sue opts out: she "does not want to be a part of any government." See characters/mary-sue.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1

## King's livestream origin of the order (institutional side)
King as a character, and the King/Keegan contradiction, belong to characters/king.md and characters/keegan-deykwein.md. This section covers only how the order came about.
- **July version (US presidency):** King "was the president of the United states," "destroyed the constitution of the auditing it," and "live streamed everything" to every citizen. Policy (the immigration debate) was resolved live between King and "the chat." Documented residents get "a vote in the American life Stream and American petitions and polls." South Korea's leaders then "started discussing the prompt that got King elected to presidency, then incorinated." Full detail in characters/king.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶16
- **2026-10-04 version (a streamer studying law):** "it essentially started with one person who decided to live stream everything. Live streamed every, everything, the entire struggle of their life. Then decided to actually start trying to impact the world." On stream: "I'm studying for my bar for, for my lawyer exam I want to understand the law what the law actually means," and chat asks "yo stream how do we do this."
- The aim: "understanding how law can be used to force everyone to help them, to help each other... That's the law. Whatever you do is for everyone. What everyone does is for everyone. So we're on the same baseline."
- Laws pass through the stream: "Together, the country signs into law because of this live stream and people spamming in the chat." When chat asks to own the house they rent, "through consensus, King is like, okay, let's figure it out. Let's do the let's do the math on that."
- Why King's power is absolute: "Humanity realized that The king should have absolute power because whoever the king is, them being a representative of the people has been so widely adopted because of its actual impact on people's lives."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- **Earliest version (robots):** King appointed by the ancient custodian robots (see forces/humans.md and engine/timeline.md).

- **Command and accountability (July):** "when king says to do something it is done afterwards that's when people confront King if they didn't like that decision." See characters/king.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶14
- **Departments:** King "created the department to handle all the people that need help."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶16
- Other named departments: the Department of Destruction (see mechanics/sick-response.md), and, in the Mars exodus, "okay, we'll need a department that manages this."

## Housing
- "We can all live in nice houses. Oh wow, how did we find the money? We shared everything. We dropped, we collectively dropped the pricing of housing. And all, all got housing. One house each. That's our home base."
- It started with chat asking "I'm renting my house. Can I just own it? I live here anyways." The rule that came out of it: own the place you live in, "Not to sell, but to live in. So that rent is not a thing anymore."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- In the cores, the cities are "a city of houses, the tallest being double or triple stories," around one large connected apartment building. See mechanics/cloning-and-family.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("A city of houses")

## Jobs
- "What if having a job is an anchor to a house? And people just spam apply. Spam apply to jobs."
- Losing your job doesn't cost you the house: "You keep the house, unless you stop applying for a job. You keep applying."
- "Losing a job is not an element of woe is me, I'm sleeping in my car tonight." It's "Uh, so I got uh, a few interviews lined up. I'm wondering if I should pivot uh, to a different branch."
- What it replaces: renting, being "on a string," "cops knock on my door. What are you doing?", and strangers harassing you "Because they don't feel connected to the people around them."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1

## Education
- "The education system is meant to just be a safe place to prepare humans to go into an environment" of work. It asks: "What were some activities where if you knew that you were being paid for what you were doing, you would have enjoyed it more."
- Example: a student good at "EGD drawings" designs a shed; it's pitched to backyard/lawn companies, "And if any of them take it, you make some money, and that's how you pay for the education."
- "You essentially take a loan out. And that loan entitles you. You can borrow against the loan to just get a house."
- The goal is for each person to find the thing that makes them feel "fuck yeah, I'm being cool. I'm a fucking cool human."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- (The 2026-10-04 message gives housing three routes in one breath: everyone got one house through shared pricing, a job anchors you to a house, and the education loan lets you borrow for a house. How they fit isn't stated; see canon/_oq/humans.md.)
- By the Mars exodus: "The average human is incredibly educated and inc- incredibly capable of Critical thinking. Efficient. And their society is fair. As fair as possible." Administrations "would need to be clear and concise and simple. Simple break, breakdowns of complicated and complex and intricate things."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
