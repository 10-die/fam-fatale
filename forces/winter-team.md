## The ships
- **The jump jet** — described as "the big ship that everyone rides in."
- **The Boeing jet** — a separate vessel, also part of the team's transport.
- Both are autonomous, behave via workflows, and have "anxiety built into them" — when Winter goes missing/silent, both ships get anxious and chase after Loading on their own initiative, without being told to. ("You're not talking to us... but they're not speaking. Their technology... can behave autonomously.")
