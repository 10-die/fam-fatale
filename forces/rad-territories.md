# RAD territories

At least two named RAD territories/polities surface in this dump. Both names come through voice transcription and may not be the final spellings — treat as provisional.

## Gucci territory
- Keegan's home territory. SICK is less normalized/"privileged" here than in McConnell territory — but Gucci's own leaders still have to go SICK sometimes, because they have to fight McConnell's leaders.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- Possibly connected to "DaGucci College," a location named once in an earlier dump's Mary Sue/Kyle hallway scene — plausible link given the shared "Gucci" name, but not confirmed by 10die. See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md and characters/kyle.md.

## McConnell territory
- Heavily SICK-saturated: "sick is everyone, sick McConnell over there... this is everywhere, it's hard to find [one] that isn't sick." The team's tech-extraction thread (Mike, Karen, Mary Sue, Keegan — see characters/mike.md) operates inside McConnell territory.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- Karen may be a McConnell herself — see characters/karen.md and OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
- 10die's own aside on how humans regard this territory: "you know how he[/they] fucking hate the McConnells but also love them" — an ambivalent relationship, not straightforward hostility. Not elaborated further in this dump.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
- The RAD leadership more broadly is described (possibly referring to McConnell specifically, possibly RAD leadership in general — not disambiguated) as "sick to the core but [a] technocratic state that has made the last 400 years of fighting brutal... for the human side."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
