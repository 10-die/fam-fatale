# RAD territories

At least two named RAD territories/polities surface in this dump. Both names come through voice transcription and may not be the final spellings — treat as provisional.

**2026-10-14 consolidation note:** later sessions spell the first territory "Deguchi," "Degucci," "Deguccis," and "degucci territory," and the July dumps say "Gucci" / "DaGucci College." 10die says outright that the name has varied but means one collective (see below). The section heading keeps the July name and adds the later spellings. Characters (Keegan, Audrey McConnell, Mary Sue, Karen, Sick) are only pointed to here; their files are under characters/.

## McConnell territory
- Heavily SICK-saturated: "sick is everyone, sick McConnell over there... this is everywhere, it's hard to find [one] that isn't sick." The team's tech-extraction thread (Mike, Karen, Mary Sue, Keegan — see characters/mike.md) operates inside McConnell territory.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- Karen may be a McConnell herself — see characters/karen.md and OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
- 10die's own aside on how humans regard this territory: "you know how he[/they] fucking hate the McConnells but also love them" — an ambivalent relationship, not straightforward hostility. Not elaborated further in this dump.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
- The RAD leadership more broadly is described (possibly referring to McConnell specifically, possibly RAD leadership in general — not disambiguated) as "sick to the core but [a] technocratic state that has made the last 400 years of fighting brutal... for the human side."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
  - **Consolidation note (2026-10-14):** the same ¶15 passage opens "and then also for address McConnell... and then one of them awdris McConnell seize what loading does." "awdris McConnell" may be an early transcription of **Audrey McConnell** (named 2026-10-10), and "address" may be "Audrey's." Not confirmed; see characters/audrey-mcconnell.md ("Possible earlier appearance").
- **Fighting culture:** McConnell territory has a televised fighting culture where combat is entertainment and spectacle. Killing each other serves as both dispute resolution and weekend recreational activity — casual violence normalized as leisure.
- **Territory danger:** Entering McConnell territory is described as "what a suicidal maniac will do" — few have tried, none have returned. The odds of survival are drastically lower than any other territory because of the saturation of active RAD champions at or near prime strength. Encountering multiple champions in prime simultaneously is a scenario humans/Gucci fighters are not equipped to handle.
- **Linked fighter consequences:** When linked fighters lose a fight in McConnell territory, the survivors wake up knocked out and disoriented — the physical link means they share the traumatic cascade of the loss, leaving them vulnerable and unable to act immediately.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18
  - **Consolidation note (2026-10-14):** ¶18 does not itself say "televised," "dispute resolution," or "weekend recreational activity"; those three phrases in the "Fighting culture" bullet are not traceable to ¶18 and should be treated as unconfirmed until 10die confirms them. ¶18's own words are: "entering McConnell is what a suicidal maniac will do. Few have tried, and none returned. You just lose yourself. Too many active RAD, like encountering multiple champions in prime. Odds drastically lower. And when linked and lose, you're knocked out and disoriented when you wake." The later sessions below do describe McConnell fights as a business and a TV show.
