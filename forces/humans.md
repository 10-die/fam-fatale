## Government structure
- Every country has its own stable government. King (see characters/king.md) functions as a leader above/across that structure, appointed originally by the ancient custodian robots (see engine/timeline.md), with humanity continuing to accept that leadership because it works. Distinct from at least one named national head of state, "the president of america Sten menli," who "flies around America in the Great bald eagle."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md — King's exact scope (national vs. supra-national) is still not fully nailed down, but this dump's split between King and a named US president leans toward King being supra-national.
- Later sessions describe the government very differently: "the governments are everyone," every member of a collective is part owner of it. That material lives in forces/human-society.md. It isn't reconciled with the "every country has a stable government" picture above; see canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1; raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1

## Global voting app
- Humans make major collective decisions via an app in which every human gets a vote; decisions wait until every human has voted, however long that takes.
- Used in arc 1 to approve the RAD-territory tech-extraction operation: after the Internet's rediscovery becomes global news, humans vote and decide to send an extraction op into RAD territory to recover as much pre-collapse tech "as we can strategically."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Compare the later decision rule (change happens when an idea wins an "overwhelming majority"; people who disagree "immediately join someone else or start their own idea") in forces/human-society.md. Logged in canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1

Consistent with 10die's own framing in his elevator-pitch request (¶3): "can the humans and the RAD learn to integrate with one another."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶3, ¶4

- The core contrast, in 10die's words: humans' "entire reason for existing is because they have to fight to live. Whereas the rad, they live to fight. They're having a blast."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("they have to fight to live")
- 10die calls rad–human coexistence "the beating heart of the story the idea that rad and human could coexist by assimilating completely. But the humans don't want to risk being sick, and the rad don't want to lose sick." (Note: July canon names the Karen/Mary Sue connection as the "beating heart"; see canon/tone-and-theme.md and canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3

  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("intellectualized their existential conflict")
- Sick can only be dealt with after it acts: "Before it happens, it's speculation. And that speculation makes, makes humans hostile towards the rat. Because that's what the king was able to do." (The last sentence is ambiguous. It could mean King turned that fear against rad, or it could just lead into the King-origin passage that follows. Logged in canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.)

### A new nation on the border (2026-10-07)
- Deguchi rad and humans start "creating a new... The thing that nationality almost. A new nation. Built from two nations, parts of two nations on the border, completely integrating one another thoroughly." They "march from front to back and just build a new territory."
- They "represent the the opposition to the sick rad and the humans who want to eliminate all rad." They start small and grow, beating the sick out of rad and then bonding rad and humans; conquered land becomes "part of their territory," and "the entire vibe and style of the civilization changes."
- Colors: "red, red, red are the Deguchi. And humans are kind of blue, silver. So they were purple together." A converted territory example: "maybe Fan 7 is now, it was sick Fan 7."
- 10die ties this to Keegan starting a Bible (see mechanics/humanism-and-the-human-bible.md). Whether this new nation is the same thing as the 2026-07-29 "third side" isn't stated; see canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 1

- What every human is owed and owes: "Every human needs to be able to fight to the death. Needs to be trained, needs to be fed, needs to be housed. Needs to be rehabilitated if they survive. Needs to be ready to defend the clones of the humans who have died."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("fuck the old order")
- The same idea in July: "everyone can fight everyone must fight," not just "when it's your shift to be a fighting agent." See mechanics/sick-response.md.

- Sick as the pressure that shapes humanity: "sick being this unstoppable force forcing humanity to become undefeatable and resilient."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶16

## Temperament
- July: people "don't talk that much" because things are so centered on fighting, "but the humans are locked in enough to know we need we need to be locked in." See canon/tone-and-theme.md for the chant-like "we are humans you must fight."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- 2026-07-29 (archive only): humans are "hardened while being very emotionally mature. Tough as nails, stoic." When something goes horribly wrong they don't break down, and "the other humans don't let them break down alone." They end up "laughing together, enjoying the moment, despite it being centered on something tragic." Acceptance of tragedy is "clear, not rushed": "Wow. That's tragic. I will miss them. We must go."
  source: archive/sessions/2026-07-29-ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick/ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick.md (no raw available)
- 2026-10-10: humans are "not insecure," "very logical." The raw says "They don't talk about like taboo things or whatever," and then "it's still taboo to think about a sick child." (The archive reads the first line as humans *not* shying from taboo topics; the raw is ambiguous.)
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("these humans are not insecure")
- 2026-10-10: humans are pleasant and approachable. They "crack a joke here, tease there," "have moments of feeling unashamed and inspired enough to push through the fear of breaking the social tension in the wrong way." "Hey, yeah, can I talk to you about this? Sure, yeah, what do you want to know about it?" Even when busy: "talk to this guy, here's his stream. He knows about it, I need to go." 10die: "If I make them human, then they become likable."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Humans are very pleasant")
- The July "don't talk much" line and the October "pleasant, approachable, joking" line pull in different directions; logged in canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.

## The border
- Adults come home "unstable" after "fighting at the border... or like fighting sick or rad or something." "They don't know what they want, but they keep trying to do things"; one gets on a bed and says "I'm uncomfortable, I don't know what I want." "Their thoughts are plagued with, with obscurity." See mechanics/cloning-and-family.md for how children "recharge" them.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
- "When humans, the sober humans, fight at the border, they can't fight forever. They eventually need to back off because they've been inundated with radiation."
- **Reflective tech:** "They wear reflective technology, but there is so much radiation in the environment." See mechanics/exo-technology.md.
- **Sleep fighting:** "So they need to stop fighting, otherwise they'll get they'll sleep fighting. They need to not fight, so they're isolated."
- Isolation "is the best way to preserve humans without having to clone them, because the cloning system is busy."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("inundated with radiation")
- Radiation inundation works through the same bonding that links rad: after a fight "you start getting their radioactive synapses, and they start shaping your mind a bit." 10die gives it as the reason border fighters can't fight forever ("That's why when humans... fight at the border"). See forces/rad.md and mechanics/family-link.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("radioactive synapses")
- **Lost to a territory:** besides knockout and death, "there's also a human getting lost to another territory. It happens." July canon already has the cloning program covering humans "killed or... lost in the rad territories."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("getting lost to another territory"); raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## Isolation and sobriety
- A border fighter carrying radiation goes into isolation. Then comes "That awful, excruciating self-damage in vain, because you cannot damage yourself and heal and like get the joys of fighting, the radiation, and yet you try anyways and your body hurts and you recover."
- "Once your room reading is zero, you're just removed and taken to a place where you can recover, you're now sober, and then you're responsible for raising clones of dead humans." Humans go to the cores "after they have isolated enough to no longer test at all on radioactive damage."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("once your room reading is zero")
- People sick has been beaten out of are also "taken to isolation." See mechanics/sick-response.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("taken to isolation")

- At the end of the first story, sick starts to die when people stop fighting while sick: "When the McConnell and the rest of the rad and even some of the humans gave up sick for good." So at least some humans used sick. (What sick is belongs to mechanics/sick.md.)
