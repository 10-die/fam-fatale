# The recharge (adults home from the border)

- **Era/arc:** not stated. Human territory, everyday life.
- **Source:** raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1. 10die: "I just had this idea while doing some deep searching in that book ... the simple vision was a bunch of adults get back home."
- **Assistant write-up (not canon):** archive/sessions/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society/post-collapse-society.md. Its "Possible direction" sections and its five scene seeds are the assistant's (see "Assistant seeds" below).

## Beats (10die's)
1. **The adults come home.** They've "just been fighting at the border ... fighting sick or rad or something. And they're, they're like unstable. They don't know what they want, but they keep trying to do things." One gets on a bed and says "I'm uncomfortable, I don't know what I want." "Their thoughts are plagued with ... obscurity."
2. **Children recharge them.** "children are sort of like recharge stations. Like, children will seek out adults to recharge them. And they essentially inject whimsy into the adults to de-disillusion them. Showing them how beautiful life can be through the eyes of a child."
3. **Children run things.** There's a lot of automation, but kids "get dinner ready together, working as kids like from young ages to like teens, and they grow up so fast because they die so frequently." They co-stream on "a personal streaming network" and work together.
4. **Sleep is the danger.** "Sometimes they have to fight back the adults. Sometimes, if they're sleeping, that's the most dangerous time because then they can just become sick. And that's when the children need to escape."
5. **The alarm and the cascade.** "the moment it becomes sick, an alarm rings in the entire human country. And it's gonna be seconds before there's a human killing agent there to land this first blow ... the first agent is probably going to lose. But then there's four agents, and then there's 12 agents, and then it just cascades."
6. **Transit.** Agents are already on trains that "zip across the country. And then you break off in a little part of yours and you slow down, slow down, slow down. Till you get to ... a terminal of your destination in a shuttle. That is designed to absorb that energy of slowing down."
7. **Erase and rebuild.** "once sick is eliminated, Destruction and destruction and rebuilding over the course of a few days ... a new blueprint had been made, and then it's built in pieces, and it's just a whole new place. And it has specific alterations, and then everyone who ... was saved is brought back. And then the next wave of respawns."
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
