- **Era/arc:** the same early era as scenes/the-field.md ("Keegan is ruthless"). No arc number.
- **Source:** raw/2026-09-27-keegan-mary-sue-combat.md, msg 1. 10die: "Okay, I just, I just thought of something insane. Okay, so this is just an idea, all right?"

## Beats (10die's)
1. **Choice and necessity.** "Mary Sue chooses to give birth to Loden. Both out of necessity to, to survive." It comes straight after the car beat (scenes/the-field.md): "maybe loading absorbs that energy to grow."
2. **Bionics trigger the birth.** "bionics are put inside her body and then it triggers a birth. And now whatever is growing is taking damage." Mary Sue is "fighting, she's running, she's moving, and she's getting pregnant. And she's kicking ass."
3. **The union.** "she doesn't let Keegan just use her for damage. She tries to survive. And forms a union with Keegan."
4. **The birth.** "when Loading is born, Keegan is there. And then Loading and Keegan start fighting. Loading gets out of the womb and attacks Keegan immediately."
5. **The march.** "everything erupts into chaos and the entire kingdom starts marching towards the center, towards this radioactive energy. The rad from the Keegans and the humans come together."
6. **Loading thinks out loud.** 10die: "I said a whole beautiful thing about how loading would say out loud ... if I die How many humans are left? Sick versus every single human on a planet? Every single one? Trying to actively take it out at the same time? Together? Sick does not get to win. Sick gets to watch as the inevitability. of the odds compounded by the variables against it, a standard deviation for creatures in their prime, in mass." Sick wonders "how I can be aware of ... this at the same time as it is ... I see it seeing me. I don't know where it is."
7. **Say it sideways.** "I want this to be more subtle, way more nuanced. Like don't say it out, don't say it like that. Like have an interesting way to say it. Because when loading and sick interact, Loading, they interact as if they've already been talking to each other, like they're on open comms. With one another. And it's scary. Sick is... Genuinely always there. If not in presence."
