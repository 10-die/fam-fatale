# Loading's first boot and the rooftop chase

- **Era/arc:** not stated. It involves Keegan, Mary Sue, Winter on a phone, and McConnells chasing them. Under the July canon Loading exists 400 years before Winter's reboot, and under 2026-10-10 Loading is built after arc 1, so where this falls is open (canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md).
- **Source:** raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18. 10die: "I'm going vivid mode, and I just wanna capture this."

## Beats
1. **Assembly.** Loading is built like a tall human skeleton, but the bones are "straight, flattish metal" with flat edges, magnetic, and they "click and lock." The head is a processor. When it goes on, "the thing just boots up. And the thing goes into kill mode."
2. **First target.** Keegan is striking it as it boots. Loading goes for Keegan first, because if Keegan escapes and hides behind fleeing humans, Loading can't kill "the optimal load of humans that are fleeing."
3. **Mary Sue runs, Winter guides.** Winter is on Mary Sue's phone: "right, left, forward, no, no, no, go, go, go, yes, left, go straight, run, there's a ladder on your left behind the wall, beyond the wall." His voice is "very thin and instantaneous."
4. **Keegan vs. Loading.** Loading shuffles, props up, "strike[s] ruthlessly." Keegan can't land a charged attack and keeps getting knocked out. Then Keegan flies at Loading mid-ascent, "like, a Doomfist punch," and smashes him against the ladder. Loading says "reassemble" and his parts move magnetically back together. Keegan grabs an ulna and a radius and flies at Loading "with his first genuine smile on his face."
5. **The rooftop.** Mary Sue, exhausted, hears "metallic stomping." On the roof she aims her phone at the sky, sees something, and clicks. Loading grabs her leg and is about to throw a fist at her skull. There's an "NFC dink notification." Winter says "stop," and Loading stops instantly.
6. **Winter moves.** Winter now speaks only inside Loading's processor. Through Loading: "I should apologize to you." Mary Sue asks for Winter on the phone. No answer.
7. **Keegan arrives.** Still climbing ("Randy Cunningham ninth-grade ninja young"), Keegan reaches the roof and starts hitting Mary Sue. "her hair and eyes start glowing" and she hits back: "stop, can you please stop just a bit, I am so tired, we just ran for our lives." She means the McConnells, not Loading.
8. **The forced fight.** Keegan "will keep hitting until Loading intervenes. And Keegan learns not to terrorize them. And it's, like, a 40-minute exhausting forced fight."
