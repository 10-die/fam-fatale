## Beats (10die's)
1. Facing a dragon, Keegan "would run away. And then the dragon would do its thing, run amok, damaging things."
2. "Then you'd hear a fighter jet. You'd see a knocked out fighter pilot Sitting in the lap of Keegan, who's flying this thing directly at the Chorza dragon." ("Chorza" is as transcribed.)
3. The pilot is knocked out and "intercepting Keegan's radiation," so "the limp body becomes animated and clicks and like clicks specific places in the plane, flips switches, and Keegan does the exact same thing."
4. A missile fires. The plane climbs "while turning."
5. "the pilot screams awake and reaches for the escape hatch and is knocked out right when their fingers touch it. And then the knocked out pilot springs back to life, this time with their eyes closed again."
6. From the dragon's side: struck in the arm, "Maybe even the wing, it can't fly." It looks for the plane, which seemed to be flying away.
7. The plane circles the dragon high up, accelerating "very unsettlingly fast." Near the half circle it turns its nose to the dragon "and just starts swooping towards the dragon and starts firing every gun and every missile and hitting every shot." It's "almost like circling a drain." "It's not an explosion there, an explosion here. Everything."
8. "right as it's about to approach, the nose tilts upwards. And then it drops a payload of bombs right on the dragon. And then as it's jetting into the sky, ripping into the sky."
9. Keegan to the scientists: "I need you to make it so that it's possible for the plane to not fall apart when it's slicing through the air sideways, so I can keep the target towards my center and hit them with everything we have on the plane and eliminate the target. I know I could just be a show of force, but I prefer to be a weapon, my own weapon."
