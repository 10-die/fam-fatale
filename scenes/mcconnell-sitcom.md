## The idea (10die's)
- "McConnell territory is like every TV show you ever seen. The lives that they live in real life. But every story has murder in it."
- The outskirts are slow: "the outskirts of the McConnell territories are where the McConnell will take their time with the kill. Interact."
- The center is fast: "The center is where all hell breaks loose and die, laser storm, laser strike, die, kill, die. Reborn."
- The look: "I imagine 2½ men, modern family. High School Musical. But instead of all of the characters fighting, they just have to kill. They fight to the death. And some don't even fight, some just kill. By surprise, ambush."
- The contrast: "that stark contrast between that and the Gucci territory, where there's a fight, there's a fight, there's a fight ... Then stop once they've had enough."
