# What would Keegan do? (Megatron, Darth Vader, the snowball)

- **Era/arc:** none. These are thought experiments about how Keegan engages. 10die wants the audience to run them too: "people on Reddit and people on YouTube talking about and making content about the idea of what would Keegan do in any fight against anything."
- **Source:** raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2.

## Vs. Megatron
- "If Keegan was standing there and saw Megatron, Keegan would start moving around restlessly. How do I get up to where Megatron is? What if I just run forward underneath the ship? If he does nothing, I'll run back and look for, for a plane and crash it into the ship." Keegan really goes: "hop over the fence right up or punch through something of an airport, a hangar."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2

## The snowball
- Keegan enters, runs toward people and away, waits "to get close to something that reacts and then fighting the shit out of it. Because once Keegan defeats it, it starts chasing Keegan and then Keegan runs to something else ... Whatever it had knocked out before is also fighting Keegan, but now also fighting with Keegan. And the fighting of Keegan is what heals Keegan."
- The beat: "you'll jump up in the air and it'll swing and grab ... your leg and ... throw its hand into the wall, locking into the wall. Keegan ... was squatting when ... all of this happened and is now in the air, rising as you do, already spinning its body to deliver a vicious kick that kicks you ... into the wall. and you bounce in front of them, like onto the ground."
- Then it spreads: "You start becoming more like Keegan and you start running in for Keegan. Running in with Keegan."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2

## Vs. Darth Vader
- Vader sees "this creature that is just getting bigger, running straight towards him," and uses the Force to stop Keegan "like a meter, like two meters away." Keegan moves to the side. Vader lifts and strangles Keegan. "you hear Keegan start making sounds and then just look at Vader not breathing and start moving around moving its body and seeing if it can make contact with the ground seeing if there's any way to break this projection of energy." "I don't know what would happen next but That's the type of interaction I would want any character who interacts with Keegan to have."
- "The first time he meets is the first time he fights, even if you're standing on a spaceship above him. Looking down at him. He runs away. and goes and does a bunch of shit then he arrives with an army he was beating people along the way."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2

## The lion
- "I want Keegan to be perceived the way a lion would ... courageous enough to engage in anything. Not out of lack of fear. But of lack of feeling fear, able to understand fear and rationalize it." Moon jumps "with a, with a thud that bounces the ground," then a dash in and a dash back "and seeing how these are how they respond."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 2
