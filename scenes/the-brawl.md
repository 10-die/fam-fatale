# The Brawl (rad music)

- **Era/arc:** not stated. Deguchi territory ("I was describing was the Deguccis").
- **Source:** raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1. It came from a kindergarten concert four houses from 10die's home: "I was thinking about what culture means."
- **Title:** "the Brawl" is the assistant's name (archive/sessions/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md). 10die doesn't name it.

## Beats (10die's)
1. "imagine the rad make music with their bodies while they fight and then together they create a cacophony of music and it's like every beat is like a piece of a different song and then it transitioned into a completely different style for a bit for a few bars."
2. From a distance: "you'd hear a fight here and a fight there in the distance knowing that there's a large group of rad there brawling and if you join them you're gonna be part of the music."
3. "they're brawling with love because they're not sick they're not trying to eliminate each other." They gain stamina as they fight, so it builds "to a crescendo where the music is, becomes very loud and then it attracts other smaller clusters."
4. The kids' concert will stop for the night. "Imagine if it doesn't stop, it just keeps building and attracting more."
5. "you'll see two different like vibes of songs like the South like Southern Twang having a fight with ... They're changing in music, changes together, and it starts to flow together. Intentionally." At first they just do damage, "but then they start understanding each other."
6. The bodies: "Sweating heaving you're literally vaping out radioactive blood in the air you're in your prime you're literally able to stop and stand still and admire something they immediately spring to action again."
7. Drifting inside it: "I'm gonna do this, you know what, whoa, I'm dro- I'm going down, a level, oh, I'm fighting these people now, that was a good kick, I dodge it."
