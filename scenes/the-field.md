## Beats (10die's)
1. **The standoff.** Keegan charges. "Mary Sue is stone cold sober. So Mary eventually like stands still. And Keegan can't get close." There's a field around her that "renders him inert when he gets close. She can break that by attacking."
2. **The chase loop behind it.** Mary Sue would "bolt, like get out, park, go over a building, gone. Keegan has to find her again." Keegan "gets very frustrated when it's quick because he barely gets to fight." He "won't stop out of a matter of principle. So Keegan tries to adapt."
3. **Both push.** When Mary Sue gets close enough she feels Keegan's radiation pushing her away, "and that's maybe something Keegan doesn't realize. They are pushing each other away."
4. **How she moves.** "She is moving like she's been infused with ballet and gymnastics and breakdancing. She's a dynamo in terms of movement. Because she's using the drugs to amplify herself." She refuses other rads' radiation because it starts "turning you into a version of them if they were you."
5. **Keegan stops.** "Keegan stops and Mary Sue gets close. And starts to feel the, the buzz" without being hit. "Mary's behavior changes. Because Mary is exhausted. She's not a rat." She doesn't regain stamina while fighting or moving.
6. **The car.** "running into a car that starts driving and being like taking a breath holy shit I'm safe and then ... your head's on the ground of the car while it's being driven by someone you trust someone you just met someone just happened to ... be in the area and you hear a car accident And the vehicle stops. and you're pulled out of the car and you're fighting again." This leads into Loading's birth (scenes/loading-born-and-open-comms.md).
7. **Learning Mary Sue.** "Keegan learns the hard way that in order to interact with Mary Sue, he needs to stop fucking around. Like actually let Mary Sue get close because when Mary Sue feels safe, she starts getting close immediately." Then Keegan is "in her company without. Actually hurting Mary Sue, but interacting with And fighting alongside."
8. **The cruel turn.** "the only way to heal Mary Sue is for Mary Sue to be beaten by Keegan. Because now they're bonding. And his energy heals, but also beats his radiation into her. And what Keegan wants is like an intense fight eventually. Wants to build up to a big fight."
