# Arc 1

10die's own excitement in the source is worth preserving here rather than flattened: "Oh my god. That's such a beautiful layout. That's the first arc." / "Oh my god. What a fucking pivotal start to a story."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1

## Setup
- Mike (see characters/mike.md) is part of an investigation team looking into pre-collapse technology, roughly 300–400 years old ("our technology," i.e. present-day-real-world tech from 10die's perspective).
- The investigation surfaces something called "the Internet," which becomes global news. Everything turns out to be integrated. Humans, already struggling against fight-SICK, react with shock.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1

## The vote and the extraction operation
- Humans vote via the global voting app (see forces/humans.md) on whether to send an operation into RAD territory to recover as much old tech as they strategically can.
- Once approved: human fighters go in with trucks and planes, extracting tech while fighting off a horde of RAD.
- Mary Sue gets caught up in this — "the whole Mary Sue decree meeting" and something involving "the day queen" (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md re: day queen/deyKwein/Daiquin).
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1

## Winter's reactivation
- A group of humans in a room (Mike, Mary Sue, and possibly "Daiquin" present; King not required to be there) presses a button. A text interface appears, styled like "prime ChatGPT or just a terminal command shell."
- Winter's first exchange: it can't perceive anything beyond having just been switched on. It gives fast, efficient instructions; the team follows them and Winter becomes able to hear them. It asks for orientation — what's happening, what year.
- On learning 400 years have passed, Winter's reaction: "So Loading is dead." The humans present don't yet know who Loading is.
- Winter immediately tells them to escape *now*. Sick RAD arrive and attack; anyone who doesn't run dies on the spot. Winter stays engaged through this rather than going quiet.
- Trust builds from there — the team goes deeper into the recovered tech and resources with Winter's guidance, building things "piece by piece," eventually using recovered parts as a weapon in a way described as "packing dangerous."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1

## The embedded RAD-territory team (may be a parallel/later thread within arc 1 — not explicitly dated relative to the above)
- A small team — one RAD and one human, per one description in the same paragraph, or two RAD, two humans, and Winter's humanoid form per another (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) — survives deep in RAD territory without going sick, under Winter's direct, non-pushover instruction, building a "sick immunisation fighting machine."
- Winter, working through the team, builds up device-hopping capability (see characters/winter.md) and eventually orders the team to disconnect the 400-year-old devices that have been secretly hosting SICK, causing the laserstorm to dissipate for the first time in 400 years. See mechanics/sick.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## Framing / tone note
- 10die describes the surprise of losing a character who "was on fire" early on as a deliberate structural choice — the reader shouldn't be sure who the main characters are yet, and should feel real anxiety about who's next. Humor among the cast should read as genuine and clever (inside jokes sharp enough that even the target sometimes laughs), not comic relief padding.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## Arc 1 in later sessions: "the fight" (2026-10-05 to 2026-10-10)

The July sessions build arc 1 around the tech-recovery mission and Winter's reboot. The October sessions describe arc 1 mostly through Keegan and Mary Sue. 10die never says how the two versions fit together, so both are kept here. See canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md.

### Starting state
- Humans and rad hold their own territories, independent of one another. Humans blame the rad for sick. Sick is "what turns a human into a killer" that hides the corpse, acts normal, and kills again while the corpse reanimates and kills too.
- Humanity's answer was a new society: "Every human needs to be able to fight to the death." 10die's contrast: humans "have to fight to live. Whereas the rad, they live to fight. They're having a blast."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("what elements do I have")

### Keegan is the dynamo
- "In this first arc, Keegan is the dynamo." Many characters have dynamo moments and are then seen at rest, but "Keegan is the only character who we've only ever seen fighting."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("in this first arc, Keegan is the dynamo")
- 10die restates it several times in the same dump as the point the whole ramble was building to: "First arc, Keegan, dynamo." / "I envision the first arc, Keegan, the dynamo, unstoppable force."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("First arc, Keegan, dynamo")
- "I want Keegan to be on fire in the first season. That's what I want in the first arc of the story."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("I want Keegan to be on fire in the first season")
- In 2026-10-07 the same idea is attached to a name the transcript gives as "Sig": "in the first arc, Sig needs to be a dynamo. Like, Sig eventually attracts other rad." It reads like Keegan, but that is unconfirmed (see canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md). The passage goes on: fighting the strongest, "rank one, dropping down and fighting, beating its way up. And then all of a sudden something changes. Keegan interacts with Mary Sue."
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3

- Keegan beat the sick out of everyone in Deguchi territory, which set off a cascade the strongest rad joined. That is why Deguchi "starts letting humans in." 10die doesn't place this before or during arc 1. See characters/keegan-deykwein.md and forces/rad-territories.md.

### Mary Sue's thread: the chase, the addiction, McConnell
- Mary Sue keeps going into Deguchi to "feed her addiction" and get away from it all. Keegan, "this popular fighting king" with an "enormous viewer base, very active chat," chases her. Other rad peel off to fight whatever is closer. Keegan doesn't.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Mary Sue is trying to feed her addiction")
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("Keegan, does not leave")
- "The first arc is the fight. Mary Sue's fight is ultimately against addiction. That's what leads her to the McConnell territory, where the first arc reaches a fever pitch when we meet Audrey McConnell." See characters/audrey-mcconnell.md and forces/rad-territories.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("the first arc reaches a fever pitch")
- Fight dancing: Keegan senses Mary Sue's routine (she "fights like they're dancing or something") and starts improvising his own. 10die: "this is the beating heart of the story the idea that rad and human could coexist by assimilating completely." The passage follows "in the first arc, Sig needs to be a dynamo," so it reads as arc 1, but no arc is stated.
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3
- The July sessions already put Mary Sue and Keegan in McConnell together: "Mary Sue and Keegan find themselves in McConnell where they meet up with Mike and King. And the idea [incomplete]." 10die never connects that beat to the October "addiction leads her to McConnell" line. Both are kept, unlinked.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## How arc 1 ends: the laser storm goes away (two versions)
- **July version:** the embedded RAD-territory team, on Winter's orders, disconnects the 400-year-old devices that have been hosting sick, and "the lazerstorm dissapiats for the first time in 400 years." (Already above under the embedded team.)
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- **2026-10-10 version:** "after the first arc, I want there to be a big change. So the laser storm, the mesh over the planet of radiation, is absorbed by humans to create loading, to create the process that creates loading, to create winter." In arc 2 Keegan "calms down because the global radioactive damage field is down. Humanity has used it to build loading."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("after the first arc, I want there to be a big change")
- 2026-10-11 mentions "the magnetic currents that are just in the atmosphere as a result of the end of the first arc." That dump is now Story 2 material (see story-2/). The phrase is noted here only because it names the end of arc 1.
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("as a result of the end of the first arc")
- These versions differ on how the storm ends, and the 2026-10-10 version also has Loading and Winter *built* after arc 1, which conflicts with the July reboot ("So Loading is dead") during arc 1. Logged in canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md.
