# Arc 3: the exodus

**About the label.** 10die never says "arc 3" in the raw. In 2026-09-30, Sick's attempt to blow up the planet is "the end of the second arc," and the exodus comes after that: "But then what if, what if I come back and I say, our planet's dying. We need to colonize Mars. And then each episode is spent where, where it's at." The same message talks about "The first story with its two arcs." The name "Arc 3 (Exodus)" comes from the assistant's session notes and the repo layout. Whether the exodus is a third arc of Story 1 or the opening of Story 2 is an open question (canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
  source: archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md

## Where the idea came from
- 10die was watching a video of a five-to-seven-year-old asked whether time goes on forever. The kid said "yeah, until the planet explodes," and then "she scared herself." 10die: "that idea seems so far out of the realm of possibility ... until Sick tries to do it."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- *(assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)* Bookend: open Story 2 with a Martian kid asked the same question, giving the same answer, and the adults going quiet. Belongs to the Story 2 pass if kept.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md

## The trigger: Sick tries to blow up the planet
- Sick realizes it will lose, "So it actually does try to blow up the planet at the end. And a large part of the chain is stopped. So it starts and then stops. But a chunk of the planet is gone."
- "And there's a danger. I don't know what what would be the danger if there's a danger of the planet drifting. That's a new idea." Raised and left open.
- "Maybe Sick dies in that explosion. Humanity defeated Sick together. That's the story." This is the end of arc 2 (see arcs/arc2.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- 2026-10-14 restates the starting point: "the population of Earth that survived the war against Sick, but the planet is destroyed and dying. So they move to Mars."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("the planet is destroyed and dying")

## Structure: one episode per stage
- "each episode is spent where, where it's at." The episodes 10die describes are below, in order.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

### Episode 1: the immediate fallout
- "the first episode, the immediate fallout. How much of the planet has been destroyed." Whoever is talking turns to "a group of people who study this stuff and they're like look we're gonna skip past that we can't survive on this planet anymore."
- The reason has to be real: "the climate is fucked the geomagnetic aspect of The planet is fried. Like, there has to be some actual existential reason that they need to leave the planet." Then: "fuck, we need to colonize Mars?"
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- *(assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)* Grounded reasons to leave: the core's dynamo is disrupted so the magnetic field collapses and the solar wind strips the atmosphere over decades; the missing chunk becomes a debris ring that falls as meteor storms; the Moon's orbit wobbles. Plus a rhyme: Mars also lost its magnetic field.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md

### The brainstorm episode
- "the world together starts brainstorming ... And then they genuinely start like brainstorming this entire thing for the whole episode."
- What's been done before: "we've made rockets before ... would we need shuttles that can stay in space? We've made that, those two."
- The Moon as a slingshot: "we, up until this point, we built things that try to push themselves through space instead of things trying to fling themselves through space and then slow down their approach."
- Crew safety: "Maybe medically induced coma?"
- Travel time, argued live: "Imagine you get you can get to Mars from the moon in a day ... A day, that's crazy. Maybe, maybe a month, but a day, maybe a month, maybe a week. We'll figure it out."
- The harder problem: "We get there, we can't breathe. We need to build structures that we can live in first ... Like trying to live under the water." No time to build an atmosphere first, but time to build hubs. Can plants grow on Mars? If not, maybe live "in the crust, under the surface. Something. And we will perish having tried."
- "Everything that ends up happening going forward is in service of that goal."
- Society on show: organizations "clear and concise and simple. Simple break, breakdowns of complicated and complex and intricate things. The average human is incredibly educated and ... capable of Critical thinking. Efficient. And their society is fair. As fair as possible."
- The chat joins in: "humans start brainstorming like, okay, we'll need a department that manages this. What are we gonna wear? What are our Martian hubs going to look like?"
- Animals: an earlier idea had animals left on Earth evolving into something, but "King wouldn't make sense as a character if they didn't consider animals. Bringing animals over to Mars. Or at least trying."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- *(assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)* Science grounding: torpor and induced coma studies, a lunar mass driver, aerocapture, perchlorates in Martian soil, lava tubes as habitats. "A day" stays the dream someone mocks in episode 1.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md

### Transit: the Moon slingshot
- The Moon as a station. At first nothing orbits it, then vehicles appear, "orbiting orbiting orbiting and then through some sort of chain reaction They will speed up and speed up ... And then they're flung." They glow as they heat up in the destination's air and slow down.
- "Overbearingly heavy shuttles and ships ... thrown through space like projectiles they're essentially catapult." They curve with the destination's orbit, crawl into low orbit, "and slide lands down the side of a mountain where it's cooled off before joining transit."
- 10die's revision in the same message: "What if the super, super, super, super, super heavy, like almost tungsten laden ships, instead, they're slingshots, and from them emerge ships that can actually land and maneuver." Space stations orbit Mars. The smaller ships look like souped-up passenger airliners, "Star Wars-ish ... the Concorde would be a style that's used."
- "these heavy, heavy ones run like one and then another one and then another one like trains. Like this is public transit by the way." There is a colony on the Moon, and people travel between Mars and the Moon.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- *(assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)* The weight is a radiation shield in transit and an ablative heat shield on arrival.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md
- 10die doesn't say whether the transit sequence is its own episode or part of the others.

### The digging episode
- "in the next episode, it cuts to them on the on the surface of Mars, literally digging, a digging operation to dig down, down, down, down, down, and then branch out."
- "the most important thing to manage is the system that is getting the ... whatever is under the ground out."
- The air drill: "a drill made of air. Powered by thermonuclear magnets. Maybe it's powered by ships ... And it's actually two ships spiraling, but not moving. And instead, their thrust, which is like rips of solid wind, rip through the air and dig dirt out of a giant hole like... A kilometer, maybe five."
- "humanity is all watching this operation that they're all invested in."
- Samples are caught and tested. The ores are "not exactly like it is on Earth," with strange reactions to magnetism: "strange minerals. Or maybe Martian minerals." What the strange metal is and does belongs to the Story 2 pass.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- 2026-10-14 gives a different drill: "massive drills, state-sized drills," used for "mining and terraforming." Whether these are a later stage or a different version of the air drill is logged in canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md and left to the Story 2 pass.
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("state-sized drills")
