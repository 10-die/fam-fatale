# Arcs

## Starting state

- Humans and rad hold **separate, independent territories.**
- Humans blame the rad for **sick**, which turns a human into a killer who hides the corpse, acts normal, and kills again. Meanwhile the corpse reanimates and starts killing too. It spreads.
- Humanity rebuilt itself around one principle: **every human must be able to fight to the death.** Rad live to fight. Humans fight to live.

## Arcs vs. seasons

**Season 1 runs for as many seasons as it takes to finish Arc 1.** It might be one season per arc, or more. Undecided.

## Arc 1: The Fight

**Keegan is the dynamo, the unstoppable force. Keegan is on fire all season.**

- Keegan has already beaten the sick out of Deguchi, which is why Deguchi is starting to let humans in.
- **Mary Sue** keeps coming into Deguchi to feed her addiction. Keegan hyperfocuses and chases her, with viewers and chat watching.
- Her addiction leads her to **McConnell territory.**
- **Fever pitch:** we meet **Audrey McConnell.**

## Between arcs

- **Humanity absorbs the laser storm.** The global radioactive damage field goes down.
- That produces **Loading**, **Winter**, and the **electron-layer** infrastructure.

## Arc 2: Sobriety

- **Keegan calms down** because the damage field is gone.
- **Mary Sue tries to go sober** with help from the whole team.
- **Something goes wrong.** TBD.
- Keegan's arc moves from *champion of beating the shit out of everything* to ***I'll punch everything at least once.***
- Loading arrives and gives **King** a remote that shuts him down.
- Thread to develop: **Mary Sue asks Loading to recover her previous clones' memories** from deep McConnell territory.
