**Martian fighter pilot. Belongs to Story 2 (the Mars era), not to fam-fatale's Arc 2.**

> Working name. The dump also hears it as "Jarvis" and "Jaws" (see [open questions](../story/open-questions.md#story-2-mars-and-jars)).

## Core

- **He never stops moving.** Other pilots, human or rad, and every other flying contraption eventually slow down, get predictable, or even stop and hover. **Jars never does.** He glides and bounces his plane through the air.
- **Momentum is never lost, only redirected.** He slides in, bounces, shoots backward, then slingshots forward. His plane changes direction without ever shedding speed.
- **He's a master of the atmosphere's magnetic currents.** He rides them the way a surfer reads water.
  - The 2026-10-11 dump said those currents came from the end of Arc 1. Since Jars lives in the Mars era, the likelier source is the **strange metal burned into the atmosphere** for energy (see [README.md](README.md#strange-metal)). The Arc 1 electron layer could be its Earth-era ancestor. *Unconfirmed.*

## The plane

- Built **partly from Martian metal**, most likely the [strange metal](README.md#strange-metal).
- **No real weapons loadout.** Other planes carry bombs, guns, knives, and things to throw or drop. **Jars rams.**
- **The nose is the weapon.** The plane's front is designed so that **it takes almost no damage on impact and all of it goes into the target.**
  - He can **fly into a building and bounce off**, while the building reacts as if the plane went straight through it.

## How he fights

- He launches himself at a target: an enemy in the air, a structure, a vehicle.
- **The enemy dodges. Jars swings around on a dime**, curving downward to cancel out the dodge, and **slams the enemy into the ground.**
- **The enemy bounces off the ground and gets hit again** on the rebound.

## In the cockpit

- **A cockpit cam shows his whole body working.** He's shaking, throwing his weight around, flipping switches, turning dials, pulling levers.
- **He isn't just steering.** He's constantly *tweaking how the plane behaves*, and that's how its momentum stays all but guaranteed.

## Lineage

He grew out of the same instinct as Keegan's fighter-jet fantasy ([scene seed](../story/scene-seeds.md#keegan-flies-a-fighter-jet)), *"I prefer to be a weapon, my own weapon,"* taken one step further. Keegan turns the plane to bring every gun to bear. **Jars makes the plane itself the weapon.**
