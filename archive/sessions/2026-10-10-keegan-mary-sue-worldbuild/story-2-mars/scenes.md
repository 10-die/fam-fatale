# Story 2 scene seeds

## The Martian kitchen

> source: `notes/raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md`

*An intense, ordinary moment. Possibly the opener that shows what Martians are.*

1. A Martian **storms into their home, yelling**, stick in hand, **conducting while demanding, almost chanting.**
2. *"I want a steak."* They throw the stick toward wherever the steak is, then snap it back to the spot right in front of them. **The steak moves from where it was to directly in front of them.**
3. They laugh. *"I want a plate."* They point at the plates, flick toward them, then flick **under the steak** and scoop. A plate flies out, swoops under the steak, and **stops dead with the wrist-stop. Clang**: the plate hits the metal.
4. At the stove they realize they need a pan. They conduct toward the pan, then **strike the point** where they want it. The pan flies there and stops.
5. Pan on the stove. *"I want butter."* Butter flings into the pan. *"Medium rare."* **The steak is medium rare, smoking.** Off the heat, onto the plate, **five-minute timer.**
6. *"I want eggs."* **Nothing happens.** The stick is passive. They haven't used it.
7. *"I want potatoes grilled, cheesy sauce all over them."* Then *"Potatoes,"* **bang**, potatoes into a pan. Some carrots too.
8. At the chopping board they look at the knife, flick at it, and say *"diced."* **The knife comes in, dices, and is thrown back where it belongs.**
9. Into the oven: *"180 degrees for an hour"* (or however long grilled potatoes take). Out again: **made, finished, cooked.**

**Craft notes:**
- The danger is built into the demo: **flick too fast and the plate breaks.** The audience learns the rule before anyone's life depends on it.
- **Step 6 is the key beat.** *"I want eggs"* with the stick idle does nothing. Speaking is the command, the flick is pressing Enter. That's the *Pseudocode vs. Bash* theme in miniature: intent alone doesn't execute.

## Jars comes to Earth

*(Mars era. Jars comes from Mars to Earth. Originally logged as fam-fatale Arc 2, moved here on 2026-10-14. Why he comes is open. See the Mars branch conflict option "Earth is still up there.")*

> source: `notes/raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md`

1. **Wide helicopter shot**, framed big enough for a Godzilla fight. But it's not monsters, it's **humans**: small, but you can make out every one of them.
2. Jars's Martian-metal jet **slides in, bounces, shoots backward, and slingshots forward.** It never loses momentum, it just changes direction, and fires itself at a target.
3. **The enemy dodges. Jars swings around on a dime**, curving downward to cancel the dodge, and **drives the enemy into the ground.**
4. **The enemy bounces back up** and gets hit again.
5. **Cut to the cockpit cam:** Jars's whole body is working. He's throwing his weight, flipping switches, turning dials, pulling levers, constantly reshaping how the plane behaves.
6. **The building beat:** the plane rams a building and **bounces off undamaged**, while the building collapses as if the plane went straight through.
7. Contrast in the same shot: every other flyer is **slowing down, going predictable, hovering.** Jars never does.
