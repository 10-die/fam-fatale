# Territories

At the start of the story, **humans and rad hold separate, independent territories.**

## Deguchi territory

- **Not sick**, thanks to Keegan, who beat the sick out of everyone there (see [../characters/keegan.md](../characters/keegan.md)).
- Because there's no sick, Deguchi **starts letting humans in.** If it were sick, humans would get torn apart.
- **Humans can live there because of the [brawl contract](rad-and-sick.md#the-brawl-contract).** The rad will fight you and won't go easy, but land a few blows and they're satisfied. Then they leave or try to bond.
- **The rhythm of Deguchi:** *there's a fight, there's a fight, there's a fight.* Everyone fights, then **stops once they've had enough.** That's the direct opposite of McConnell.
- Its top fighters are called **"kings"** or maybe **"killers."** Undecided; "killers" is the current lean.
- **The cycle:** weaker rad eventually get bored of casual, human-style fighting, get more aggressive, and fight their way to the top just as one of the killers goes down. *Every rad eventually gets a shot at the top.*
  - Bottom tier: **gym fights.**
  - Top tier: **Colosseum marathons of war.**
- Fights are watched: Keegan has **an enormous viewer base and a very active chat.** Streaming culture exists on both sides.

## McConnell territory

- **So sick that Audrey McConnell has beaten himself into every McConnell.** (See [../characters/audrey-mcconnell.md](../characters/audrey-mcconnell.md).)
- Every McConnell looks like a sibling: **very little variation in facial structure, complete variation in race and gender.**
- **Not a hive.** They're individuals, but they're all *the same individual.* They can send radioactive data to each other, but they'd rather just kill each other.
- **No bottom tier.** Everyone is at the top and equal, and Audrey is the strongest. **They're all dangerous.**
- **Tone: fun, not dreary.** Not a corporate, mechanical sludge-fest of horrors.

### Every TV show you've ever seen, plus murder

- McConnell territory looks like **every TV show you've ever seen**: *Two and a Half Men*, *Modern Family*, *High School Musical*. They live those lives for real.
- **Every story has a murder in it.** The characters don't just fight, they **have to kill.** They fight to the death.
- **Some don't even fight.** They kill by surprise or ambush.
- Contrast with Deguchi: Deguchi rad fight until they've had enough. McConnells don't stop until someone is dead.

### Geography: outskirts vs. center

- **The outskirts are slow.** This is where McConnells **take their time with the kill.** They interact first. The sitcom lives play out here.
- **The center is where all hell breaks loose.** *Die, laser storm, laser strike, die, kill, die, reborn.* It's a nonstop death-and-rebirth churn under the vortex cloud (see [rebirth rule](rad-and-sick.md#death-and-rebirth-the-red-cloud)).
- Deeper still: the weird sick ones that humans can't see (see below).
- Mary Sue's addiction leads her here. **Arc 1 reaches its fever pitch here** when we meet Audrey.
- **"The McConnells are the ones humans can see."** Deeper inside, the territory gets stranger: *"there's some weird ones deep inside, bro."* They're sick. That's also where many of **Mary Sue's past clones** ended up.
