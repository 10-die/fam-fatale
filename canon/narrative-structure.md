# Narrative structure

How Story 1 is told: whose eyes we're behind, how the camera moves, and how the arcs and seasons are cut. For tone and recurring design principles, see canon/tone-and-theme.md. For the arcs themselves, see arcs/. For scenes, see scenes/README.md.

## No protagonist, no antagonist
- July: "it's not protagonist vs Villian it's a clash of competing forces."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶3

  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("this story has no protagonist or antagonist")

## The POV relay
10die, 2026-10-10: "I want to be consistent about this."
1. "We follow this person in this group, and they interact with the person, and then we change perspective. Now we're hanging out with that person, seeing what they're doing."
2. "through the group, we move, and we start to understand their bond, and see them work together."
3. "they interact with another group. We see them. We see the faces. We see some behaviors."
4. "Then they interact with another group. And then something happens."
5. "at some point we go back to the first character we followed. And their conflict, they're like close to another member of the second group."
6. "maybe, I don't know, one defeats the other. I don't know which yet. I guess it doesn't really matter."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("we change perspective")

## Staying with the loser
- "maybe the second group gets defeated, that guy gets defeated, and then the character we've been following, of the family we've been following, leaves. And then we stay with that knocked out poor guy who tried. You saw them try, but they got cooked. Maybe they tried to do something and made an error."
- "we see their group, their family, their whatever, come to them and comfort them and hold them ... And they start shit talking the other group. And we know that some of them are rumors, but we know some of them are true. And it's like, oh shit, oh shit. We're shit talking the group we were just following. We kind of like those guys. I guess whether we like them or not is subjective. That's a beautiful part."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("we stay with that knocked out poor guy who tried")
- The balcony scene is a POV that falls with the loser: the camera goes over the balcony with the human who got kicked and learns of the other death from the kill feed. See scenes/the-balcony.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

## Pacing devices
- **Keegan as a pending event.** "please, please, somebody, don't invite Keegan yet. Like, is Keegan coming? Like, literally, like, if Keegan was here right now, all of this yapping, all of these insults, it would just be fists. And when Keegan shows up, it's fists."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("don't invite Keegan yet")
- **Cold opens and episode-ending stings.** The "Winter's missing" episode ends on a view from space of something heading toward Canada: "the episode ends. Boom. Cold open. New episodes awaiting this motherfucker." See arcs/arc2.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- **A reveal at the top of an episode.** Loading's jet is noticed by the team at the start of an episode, not announced. See canon/tone-and-theme.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
- **Cross-episode reveals about sick.** A corpse jumps out a window at night, "And in the next episode, we see that character."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("in the next episode, we see that character")
