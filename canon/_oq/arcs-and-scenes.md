## Arc boundaries and what each arc is

- **How the laser storm goes away, and when.** In the July sessions the embedded RAD-territory team disconnects the 400-year-old devices hosting sick, and "the lazerstorm dissapiats for the first time in 400 years." This sits inside arc 1. In 2026-10-10, "after the first arc ... the laser storm, the mesh over the planet of radiation, is absorbed by humans to create loading," and that is why Keegan calms down in arc 2. Both versions end the storm around the end of arc 1, but the mechanism differs (disconnecting devices vs. absorbing the storm). 2026-10-10 is later.
