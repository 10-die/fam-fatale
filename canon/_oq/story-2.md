# Open questions — Story 2 (Mars era)

Fragment for `canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. Canon for this area lives in `story-2/`.

## Contradictions (both versions kept in canon)

- **Which story Jars belongs to.** 2026-10-11: Jars is in fam-fatale's "second arc," coming "from the Martian colony of humans" to Earth, which implies a Martian colony already exists during Story 1's arc 2. 2026-10-14 (later): "That was part of another story... where humans colonized Mars and they all went there. And then Jars is alive in that time." The later session reads as an explicit correction, but 10die says "I have to reconsider this," so it's logged rather than closed. (The 2026-10-10 archive notes also record dropping an Arc-2 Martian colony.)
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("I've just been envisioning the second arc")
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("That was part of another story")
  Later session: 2026-10-14.

- **The drills: air drill vs. state-sized drills.** 2026-09-30: the first dig on Mars uses an air drill — "two ships spiraling, but not moving," thrust "like rips of solid wind" blowing out a hole "A kilometer, maybe five." 2026-10-14: the survivors "build massive drills, state-sized drills, and start mining and terraforming." Two stages of the same operation, or two competing versions? Not said.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("state-sized drills")
  Later session: 2026-10-14.

- **Who uses strange metal and bash commands: everyone, or one faction?** 2026-09-30 frames it as Martians generally: "humans use strange minerals," "humans have use metal they... sharpen the metal into like long sticks," "they can use bash commands." 2026-10-14 splits humanity: some "stuck with the old technology," and the new-tech side, working with AI, "created bash commands." Whether the stick and bash commands are universal by Story 2's time or belong to the new-tech side isn't stated.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("some humans stuck with the old technology")
  Later session: 2026-10-14.

- **Where the magnetic currents Jars rides come from.** 2026-10-11 (when Jars was still placed in fam-fatale): the currents are "in the atmosphere as a result of the end of the first arc." 2026-10-14: the Mars-era atmosphere holds electrons "from all the strange metal that gets mined and burned into the atmosphere." 10die never connects the two. If Jars is Mars-era, which source applies?
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("as a result of the end of the first arc")
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("burned into the atmosphere")
  Later session: 2026-10-14.

- **Who goes to Mars, and who's left on Earth for Jars to fight?** 2026-10-14: "humans colonized Mars and they all went there"; it's "the population of Earth that survived the war against Sick" (which could include rad, since 2026-09-30 says "humans and rats" stopped fighting while sick). But 2026-10-11 has Jars fighting on Earth among "other human or rad pilots or flying contraptions." Who are the enemies on Earth, do rad exist in the Mars era, and why does Jars go?
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("they all went there")
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("other human or rad pilots")
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
  Later session: 2026-10-14.

- **Heavy slingshot ships: slide-land, or carriers for smaller ships?** Same message, self-revised. The revision probably wins, but 10die didn't say the first version is dropped.

  Later: the second version, same message.

- **Animals: left on Earth to evolve, or brought to Mars?** Same message, self-revised. First: "they left animals on Earth and they just ended up evolving into something." Then: "King wouldn't make sense as a character if they didn't consider animals. Bringing animals over to Mars. Or at least trying." (Also touches the King vs. Keegan contradiction tracked elsewhere: this passage assumes King is a separate leader during the exodus.)

  Later: the second version, same message.
