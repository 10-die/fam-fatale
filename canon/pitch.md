# Pitch

**Provenance note:** everything in this file is the assistant's in-session synthesis from a single chat exchange, not 10die's own wording. 10die asked for it and didn't visibly object to it in the rest of the dump, so it's worth keeping as a reference draft — but treat it as a first-pass pitch to rewrite, not settled canon. 10die's own follow-up message was literally "i wanna rewrite that."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶3–¶4

## 10die's own framing (10die's words, not the AI's)
> "a story with violence not gore, everyone fights, and everyone is loved by their family and their group, it's not protagonist vs villain it's a clash of competing forces, can the humans and the RAD learn to integrate with one another or will sick finally get it's unending global utopia of wars?"
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶3

## Draft logline (AI-drafted, unconfirmed)
"Four hundred years after our world burned, humanity keeps the peace with an ancient AI split into two halves — a blade that never stops optimizing, and the soul that decides what's worth aiming at. Across the border, an entire species is addicted to a war-high so literal that saying its name can trigger it. The question isn't who wins. It's whether either side can stop before there's nothing left to fight for."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶4

## Draft "why they say yes" bullets (AI-drafted, unconfirmed)
- "Your cold open is a terminal typing 'So Loading is dead' to people who don't yet know who that is — and then killing the ones who don't run."
- "Your season finale is a Japanese city proving integration actually works — a hybrid team, human and RAD, fighting side by side — the same week your fallen hero proves the addiction might be stronger than the cure." (10die never mentions a Japanese city. This is the assistant's invention.)
- "Every power is earned, every fight is legible, and every single person on screen belongs to somebody."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶4

## Draft pitch body (AI-drafted, unconfirmed)
"Everyone fights, because fighting is how this world stays alive — but nobody dies clean. You get banged, not killed, and what happens while you're down is half the drama. Humanity survived collapse by building Loading, a machine built to eliminate every threat, and Winter, the half of him that figured out a knife with nothing left to sharpen itself against just starts inventing enemies. Across the wire, the RAD aren't monsters — they're junkies. SICK turns violence into a high, and some of them just want to keep taking hits until it kills them. This isn't good guys and bad guys. It's two families — human and RAD — both trying to survive the same addiction from opposite sides of it."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶4

Note: this draft's "SICK is an addiction" framing should be read alongside mechanics/sick.md's tech-hosted framing from later in the same dump — see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, they aren't confirmed to be reconciled.
