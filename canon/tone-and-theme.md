# Tone & theme notes

Distinct from canon/pitch.md (which is an AI-drafted synthesis 10die asked for and hasn't confirmed). This file is 10die's own direct statements about how the story should *feel*, worth keeping visible on their own rather than buried in character files.

## Dread, cut with real humor — not comic relief
- The setting should read as genuinely dreary/dark ("the dread of this world... should be completely dreary"), but 10die is explicit that humor has to survive inside that: "the thing that keeps this [from being unbearable is] humor[ous] observations." This matches the harness doc's own established texture note about inside jokes being "so clever the target sometimes laughs."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## The "beating heart" — Karen and Mary Sue
- Explicitly named as the story's emotional anchor: "I'm thinking that Karen and Mary Sue [have] some sort of connection... I hope will be viewed as a beating heart in this story, some element of consistency in the chaos." See characters/karen.md and characters/mary-sue.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## "Family" is who you survive with, not who you were born to
- Directly tied to the cloning/respawn system (see mechanics/cloning-and-family.md): humans group into families that are explicitly not blood family — "the family you respawned with." 10die's own framing: someone dies, their family is gone, and they respawn among other people — that becomes their family. This is presented as the emotional logic underneath the Karen/Mary Sue beat above, not a separate idea.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## How a fight-obsessed culture talks
- With things "so [centered] on fighting," people don't talk much day-to-day, but there's enough shared conviction to rally around short, aggressive phrases — 10die's own example, preserved as spoken: "knocking the fuck out, killing, kill it, we are humans, you must fight." Read as chant-like unity language rather than ordinary dialogue.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12

## SICK is dark on purpose — see mechanics/sick.md
10die names the tone directly while defining the mechanic itself: "this story is dark." The "SICK is a workflow" framing (full intelligence retained, intent turned malicious) is the mechanical expression of that darkness, not a separate theme note — cross-reference rather than duplicate here.

## Rad live to fight. Humans fight to live.
- "Their entire reason for existing is because they have to fight to live. Whereas the rad, they live to fight. They're having a blast. Even in the sick territory, they're having so much fun. It's ridiculous. And I think that's what's going to be the stark contrast."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("they live to fight")

## Horror without gloom (and how it sits with "dreary")
- On McConnell, the sickest territory: "maybe people would immediately go to, okay, McConnell territory is probably some dark, dreary, dreadful, corporal, mechanical, sludge fest of horrors. No." That is the last word of the dump.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("sludge fest of horrors. No.")
- McConnell as sitcom: "McConnell territory is like every TV show you ever seen. The lives that they live in real life. But every story has murder in it ... I imagine 2½ men, modern family. High School Musical. But instead of all of the characters fighting, they just have to kill." See scenes/mcconnell-sitcom.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-mcconnell-sitcoms-and-the-brawl-contract.md ("like every TV show you ever seen")
- "Beautifully strange": children in a crisis society who are still children. "it would be beautifully strange to see children in, in in this element, still being children, still having like, like fun, and they manage the stress by by working together."
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
- This pulls against the July line that "the dread of this world ... should be completely dreary" and "this story is dark." Both are kept. In July the darkness is in the world and in sick, and humour is what keeps it bearable. In October the darkness is never gloomy in feel. Logged in canon/_oq/arcs-and-scenes.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## Violence, not gore
- "a story with violence not gore, everyone fights, and everyone is loved by their family and their group." See canon/pitch.md. Compare CLAUDE.md rule 5 (damage is "banged," fights are legible) and mechanics/damage-and-combat.md (a sword to the neck does damage, not death).
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶3

## Recurring design principles
These recur across sessions. The headings "the tool is the weapon," "momentum is character," "show it, never announce it," "horror without gloom" and "scale you can read" come from the assistant's 2026-10-10 write-up (archive/sessions/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-worldbuild/story/design-principles.md). The evidence under each is 10die's.

### The tool is the weapon
- Jars's plane (Story 2) rams a building, takes almost no damage, and the building behaves as if the plane went through it, "using the plane as the weapon. And that's the type of thinking I want in this story, in this show, in whatever it ends up being." The wording covers more than Story 2.
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("using the plane as the weapon")
- Keegan in a fighter jet: "I know I could just be a show of force, but I prefer to be a weapon, my own weapon." See scenes/keegan-flies-a-fighter-jet.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("I prefer to be a weapon, my own weapon")
- Loading's finger gun "fires a punch, essentially. And Loading uses this for movement sometimes to displace himself and others."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("it fires a punch, essentially")
- A greeting can be a fist. Where humans greet by sending energy toward someone to be perceived, "Keegan does so with their fist."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Keegan does so with their fist")

### Momentum as character
- Keegan is always seen moving toward a fight: "why are they moving? Towards a fight." He is "very, very, very difficult to dodge," and Mary Sue "can dodge everything." 10die calls that pairing the thing that makes her interesting.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Mary Sue can dodge everything")
- Loading has to time his wings: "he should try to use them and just think about when he uses them. Maybe there's even elements of him calling them too soon or too late that he researches."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("calling them too soon or too late")
- In Story 2, Jars "never stops moving," and the Martian stick needs finesse ("If you do it too fast, you can break your plate"). See story-2/.
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("Jaws never stops moving")
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("you can break your plate")
- Rad movement as a friction variable: glide, then high friction, "a turn immediately keeping momentum unless they strike and they strike with that momentum." See mechanics/rad-movement.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

### Show it, never announce it
- "We're not told that Keegan's the greatest from the first chapter. That's cheating. We see the story as usual, but the impact of Keegan is undisputable."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("That's cheating")
- Loading's jet: "this is not a big announcement to show them ... look what I've built it's a wait a minute we are ... in the jet that wasn't there last time oh snap wait that's cool that's new." See characters/loading.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15
- Loading's inevitability line about Sick: "I want this to be more subtle, way more nuanced. Like don't say it out, don't say it like that. Like have an interesting way to say it."
  source: raw/2026-09-27-keegan-mary-sue-combat.md, msg 1
- "No expositional yapathons." Betrayal shown as silent rage, with no "I CAN'T BELIEVE YOU." Dialogue stays natural.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-07-29-ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick/ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick.md (no raw available)
- Sparse dialogue around Keegan: "Keegan responds with his fists and communicates with damage. Dialogue is sparse, like, as few words as possible."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

### Fights long enough for everyone to try
- "I want this to be a story where because of how it's written fights are able to last multiple moments so you see each individual in the fight get that opportunity to, to try." Committed moves like the drop strike leave the attacker open, and that makes room for it.
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

### Scale you can read
- In the Jars scene (Story 2): framing "wide enough to process a Godzilla fight, but it's not Godzilla, it's humans, and they're kind of small, but you can see them," then a cam inside the cockpit showing the body working. 10die doesn't say whether this framing applies to Story 1 too.
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("wide enough to process a Godzilla fight")

## Everyday texture (July 29, no raw)
- Pauses. People eating while they talk, "actually trying to feed themselves, not seated stiffly around a dinner table." Sometimes a dinner in near silence "with nothing awkward about it."
- Humans "don't let them break down alone." They end up "laughing together ... despite it being centered on something tragic." The acceptance of tragedy is "clear, not rushed": "Wow. That's tragic. I will miss them. We must go."
- Characters are "not maliciously insecure. They are dangerously, violently righteous."
  source: archive/sessions/2026-07-29-ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick/ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick.md (no raw available)

## Themes 10die states outright
- **Fighting to kill vs. fighting for love.** "Sick represents fighting to kill. The Deguchi represent Fighting for the love of fighting. Fighting for the love of bonding, for understanding, for experimenting."
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3
- **Humanity defeated Sick together.** "That's the story." Sick only starts to die when people stop fighting *while sick*. "Sick is itself an object that views everything as an object." See arcs/arc2.md.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Culture as collective personhood.** "Culture. is the personhood of a collective to agree to keep the whole neighborhood pumping with music because they're enjoying something together." This is where the Brawl comes from (scenes/the-brawl.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1
- **Growth.** "I love growth, to be able to follow someone as they grow."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("I love growth")
