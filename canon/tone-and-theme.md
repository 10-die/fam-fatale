# Tone & theme notes

Distinct from canon/pitch.md (which is an AI-drafted synthesis 10die asked for and hasn't confirmed). This file is 10die's own direct statements about how the story should *feel*, worth keeping visible on their own rather than buried in character files.

## Dread, cut with real humor — not comic relief
- The setting should read as genuinely dreary/dark ("the dread of this world... should be completely dreary"), but 10die is explicit that humor has to survive inside that: "the thing that keeps this [from being unbearable is] humor[ous] observations." This matches the harness doc's own established texture note about inside jokes being "so clever the target sometimes laughs."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## The "beating heart" — Karen and Mary Sue
- Explicitly named as the story's emotional anchor: "I'm thinking that Karen and Mary Sue [have] some sort of connection... I hope will be viewed as a beating heart in this story, some element of consistency in the chaos." See characters/karen.md and characters/mary-sue.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## "Family" is who you survive with, not who you were born to
- Directly tied to the cloning/respawn system (see mechanics/cloning-and-family.md): humans group into families that are explicitly not blood family — "the family you respawned with." 10die's own framing: someone dies, their family is gone, and they respawn among other people — that becomes their family. This is presented as the emotional logic underneath the Karen/Mary Sue beat above, not a separate idea.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## How a fight-obsessed culture talks
- With things "so [centered] on fighting," people don't talk much day-to-day, but there's enough shared conviction to rally around short, aggressive phrases — 10die's own example, preserved as spoken: "knocking the fuck out, killing, kill it, we are humans, you must fight." Read as chant-like unity language rather than ordinary dialogue.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12

## SICK is dark on purpose — see mechanics/sick.md
10die names the tone directly while defining the mechanic itself: "this story is dark." The "SICK is a workflow" framing (full intelligence retained, intent turned malicious) is the mechanical expression of that darkness, not a separate theme note — cross-reference rather than duplicate here.
