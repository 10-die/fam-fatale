# Sick: the mind in many minds, the kill transfer, and the storm

Brainstorm, 2026-10-05 (part 3). Follows on from `2026-10-05-keegan-and-the-sick.md`.

---

## 1. Sick is one character in many minds

- **Sick lives in multiple minds at once.**
- **How:** it adds a layer to the synapses a creature already has, an *extra* set of synapses under Sick's influence, and rewires the creature.
- **The result:** the creature becomes almost **magnetically drawn to the chance of a kill.**
- This fits part 2. Keegan's sibling is the mind Sick has been in **longest and deepest**, so long that the two have fused. "Sick" is the name of both the sibling and the thing living in them. (Open: is the sibling Sick's *origin*, or just its oldest host?)

## 2. Why kill: the transfer

- When something dies **as fast as possible**, there's a moment when energy transfers.
- **Radiation doesn't disappear.** The rad that made the kill absorbs it.
- **The dead rad now streams its killer.** It watches through the killer's eyes and can **chat to them**.
  - This explains the earlier "death isn't final" idea, where you're stuck watching whoever beat you, and probably the **kill feed** from the balcony scene too.
- **The chat rooms are diabolical.** Toxic, vile, awful. The most unforgivable things get said there. Every killer carries an audience of everyone they've killed, all of them furious.

### Damage stacks
| Kills | Damage |
|---|---|
| 1 | ×2 |
| 2 | ×3 |
| n | ×(n+1) |

- **When a killer dies, their killer gets everything:** that rad *and* everything it had absorbed, chat room included. **No one gets left behind.** It's a chain of transfers.
- Over time the radiation pools into fewer and fewer rad.

## 3. The final wars

- In the end only a few rad are left, and their radiation is **off the charts, like literal nuclear bombs.**
- What it looks like: tapping, sliding, dashing, punching, throwing each other into buildings, **dropping entire structures on each other**, flying through the ground and the air with every leap, **cartwheels that spin and tear up the ground**, kicks that rip up the dirt and end in another kick.
- **Truly epic, parable-level brawling.**

## 4. The end: lightning and the storm

1. The last rad kills the last other rad.
2. **Lightning strikes the final rad.** Inside the strike they are destroyed, *into the sky*.
3. **The laser storm begins to rain.** The storm itself tears, bends, and rips, throwing itself into itself.
4. What falls are **massive chunks of water, whole seconds' worth of water at once**, hurled at the ground. They do untold damage and destroy buildings and structures.

So all the stored radiation of every rad ever killed goes back into the world as weather.

## 5. Rebirth: the storm brings everyone back

- **The laser storm rebirths rad everywhere.** Death really is temporary. The storm is the reset.
- **This happened in the prime era, when every rad territory was sick.** Sick is the **conduit** that lets rad come back from the dead. *(Maybe framed as sacrifice. Still undecided.)*
  - So the sick aren't just violent. They're the reason anyone returns. That's why sick territories can argue they're necessary, and why refusing sick (like the Degucci do) costs something.

### How a rad is reborn
1. Rad bodies are **thrown from the sky** in the storm.
2. They **form on the way down.**
3. They hit the ground, and **the impact damage puts them back together.** They become fully real at that moment.
4. **They come back as kids.**

### Growing up: fighting is aging
- **To age, they have to fight.** They have that fighting nature in them from the start, so the kids just want to fight each other, and they do.
- Some kids go looking for sick on purpose.
- **Sick spreads through the kids' fights.**

**Where it landed (after thinking it through):**
- ~~First version: a kid who gets sick ages rapidly mid-fight and just becomes an adult.~~
- **Final version:** a kid who gets sick **young stops aging. They stay a child.** But **when they fight and do damage, they become huge adults.** Child at rest, giant in the fight.

## Open questions (part 3)

1. ~~**Does death stay temporary?**~~ *Yes. The storm rebirths everyone (section 5).* Part 1 said the dead "come back with a vengeance." Part 3 has the dead streaming inside their killer, passed down the chain until the final rad. Do they come back *out of the storm*? Is the storm the reset that brings everyone back and starts the cycle again?
2. ~~**Is the storm a cycle or the apocalypse?**~~ *A cycle. It's the reset.* If it repeats, it could be the world's seasons (feudal republics → recessions → final wars → storm).
3. **Does Sick want the final wars?** If Sick is one mind in many bodies, then every sick-vs-sick kill is Sick feeding itself. Is the endgame Sick consolidating into one body?
4. **Can the chat influence the killer?** The dead are diabolical and want revenge. Can they drive their killer toward their own death, or into the arms of a rival?
5. **What about Keegan?** Keegan kills and isn't sick. Does Keegan carry a chat room? What do the dead say to someone who never wanted the kill?
6. **"Fast death" requirement.** Does a slow death transfer less, or nothing? That would push the sick to kill as quickly as possible, which explains the drop strike and the lethal style.
7. **Sick-young kids:** what do they look like *between* fights? Childlike minds in a child's body, or Sick's ancient mind in a child's body? (Creepy either way.)
8. **Do non-sick kids grow up normally** by fighting, slowly? Is that how the Degucci raise their young, through the Brawl?
9. **If sick is the conduit for rebirth,** does a Degucci who dies sober still come back? If they do, it's because of a sick system they refuse to join. If they don't, refusing sick means risking permanent death.
10. **Sacrifice:** who or what is sacrificed so the rest come back? Is it the final rad, the one destroyed in the lightning?
