# fam-fatale

Story bible for **fam-fatale**, plus its sequel, **Story 2**, set on Mars.

> **Rad live to fight. Humans fight to live.**
> "A story with violence not gore, everyone fights, and everyone is loved by their family and their group. It's not protagonist vs villain, it's a clash of competing forces."

**Read [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) before adding anything.** The rules: raw stays raw, every canon entry cites its source, and contradictions get logged, not quietly resolved.

### History

From July to October 2026 the story was brainstormed across eight separate sessions. Each one worked on its own branch, and none could see the others. On 2026-10-14 all eight were merged here:

1. The raw words were recovered from the session transcripts.
2. Everything was mined into one canon with citations.
3. The contradictions were logged in `canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

The Jars material from claude.ai chats, and any raw for the 2026-07-29 session, were never in the repo. Add them to `raw/` to bring them in.
