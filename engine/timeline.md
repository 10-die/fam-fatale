# Timeline

## The 400-year gap
- The present-day story sits roughly 400 years after "our" world/technology (i.e., the real world as of 10die's own present) — described consistently as "300 to 400 years ago, our technology" and "four hundred years in the future" from Winter's activation-era perspective.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Ancient robots from "our time" positioned themselves as custodians of humanity after some unspecified collapse, and appointed King as leader of the group humans now organize under.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Winter and (implicitly) Loading predate the present story by the full 400 years — Winter's reactivation scene has it realize "so Loading is dead" purely from being told the current date, meaning Loading existed and Winter went dormant/was lost at some point during that gap. See characters/winter.md and OPEN_QUESTIONS.md (Loading's activation timeline) — how this reconciles with Loading appearing active elsewhere is not resolved.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Pre-collapse tech (phones, laptops, a "network interface card," possibly smart watches/glasses) survives scattered across the world, kept charged and functional this entire time by Winter secretly maintaining it. See characters/winter.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- SICK/the laserstorm has been running, tech-hosted, for the same 400 years, until the embedded RAD-territory team disconnects the hosting devices. See mechanics/sick.md.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
