## The hallway/orbit scene
- Setting: a hallway at "DaGucci College" (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md — name only appears once, in passing).
- Kyle passes Mary Sue in the hallway while walking with the rest of the family (parents, other siblings) ahead of him. Kyle stops, turns to face Mary Sue — now facing the *opposite* direction from the family — and starts walking toward her, effectively anchoring/holding back the whole family's forward momentum.
- This demonstrates the family "orbit": if a linked RAD in a family unit tries to pull away while the others keep moving, the pull physically drags the rest of the family backward/toward them — their feet slide on the floor as they're dragged. It shows the parents/older family members are also linked, and shows they weren't thrilled about it ("get pissed").
- Kyle is outnumbered by his own family in this stand-off, "but just barely" — framed as a deliberate confrontation, not an accident: "we're gonna crawl forward, but fuck you, I'm gonna confront this."
