## Trafficking and blood addiction history
- Mary Sue was trafficking humans for stronger blood — she wanted the strongest refined blood, engaging in "pseudo-heraldic mixing between regular humans and red RAD." She was "doing fucked up shit" to acquire it.
- Present addiction: feeding after stronger blood. This is a core drive for her, tied to her exhaustion and survival instincts during high-stress scenarios.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Rooftop chase and NFC activation moment
- During an escape from McConnell RAD, Mary Sue is in pursuit by Loading (who is in kill mode). She's exhausted from running and panicking at the sound of Loading's metallic stomping behind her.
- Mary Sue reaches a rooftop and frantically aims her phone at the sky, sees something, and clicks — activating an NFC signal.
- Loading grabs her leg as she tries to escape. Loading is about to strike a killing blow at her skull when the NFC signal triggers and Winter says "stop" — Loading stops instantly.
- At this moment, Winter transitions from being on Mary Sue's phone to being directly inside Loading's processor. Winter then says to Mary Sue (through Loading): "I should apologize to you." Mary Sue asks for Winter back on the phone, but Winter is now only speaking to Loading internally — Winter is no longer responding on the phone.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Winter as guide during escape
- Winter provides real-time navigation to Mary Sue while she's running from pursuit: "right, left, forward, no, no, no, go, go, go, yes, left, go straight, run, there's a ladder on your left behind the wall, beyond the wall." Winter's voice is described as "very thin and instantaneous" — rapid-fire commands without hesitation.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Relationship with Keegan and teaching role
- After reaching the surface (escaping Loading), Keegan arrives on the rooftop, having climbed up rapidly ("Randy Cunningham ninth-grade ninja young"). Keegan's communication is sparse — dialogue minimal, he responds with his fists and communicates damage.
- Mary Sue is still exhausted from the chase (referring to McConnells chasing them, not Loading). When Keegan starts hitting her, her hair and eyes start glowing, and she hits back while saying: "stop, can you please stop just a bit, I am so tired, we just ran for our lives."
- Keegan's behavior with Mary Sue is response-dependent — he can go hostile to docile. He will keep hitting until Loading intervenes. Over time, after learning not to terrorize them through exhausting 40-minute forced fights, Keegan learns from Mary Sue.
- Mary Sue teaches Keegan what he despises most: running from fate, being afraid to fight, losing, not always fighting. She almost forces him to learn the fighting lesson but doesn't — she teaches through example and through setting limits.
- Keegan gives Mary Sue "drugs she needs to keep going" — inhalants/vaping that cause an inebriated effect described as "breath of fresh air with every strike you land, surge of dopamine and euphoria, keep going." This is a survival trade: Mary Sue gets chemical support to keep moving; Keegan gets an opponent who won't flee, teaching him to engage without killing.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18
