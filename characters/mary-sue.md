# Mary Sue

**Addict. Dodges everything. Carries the Arc 2 sobriety fight.**

## Core

- **She dodges everything.** Keegan is almost impossible to dodge. Mary Sue is almost impossible to hit. That's the axis between them.
- **Emotionally unavailable.** She spends her time alone.
- **Her fight is against addiction.** Arc 1 is *the fight*. Mary Sue's fight is ultimately with herself.
- **She doesn't want to be reborn.** Rebirth would cost her **her memories**, and she'd **have to be sober.**
  - Working read: like any human, her clones wake with no memories. Her addiction is to fighting/radiation, the same pull that makes border fighters "sleep fight." *(Confirm: see [open questions](../story/open-questions.md).)*

## What she wants

- The rest of the world says *family is whoever I'm around.* **Mary Sue doesn't.** She doesn't want to be shuffled between families. **She wants to find people she can belong with for a long time, maybe forever.**
- **She could ask Loading to recover the memories of her previous clones.** Many of those clones are **deep in McConnell territory**, past the McConnells humans can see, where the weird sick ones are.
- The red cloud is a virtual server where rad view the perspective of the rad or humans they recently interacted with. The dump says *"that's where Mary Sue comes in."* That could be how lost memories survive. *(Undeveloped.)*

## Arc 1

- She keeps going into **Deguchi territory** to feed her addiction.
- She fights the local rad and escapes. They follow briefly, then peel off to fight whatever's closer.
- **Keegan doesn't peel off.** Keegan hyperfocuses and chases her, in front of a huge viewer base and an active chat. She's fed up and harassed under threat of assault. She doesn't want any part of the popular fighting king.
- **She knows the rules and uses them.** The other strong Deguchi rad hunt her too, but she can count on another rad's punch to pull them into a new fight. **That trick doesn't work on Keegan.**
- Her addiction eventually **leads her to McConnell territory**, where Arc 1 hits its fever pitch.
