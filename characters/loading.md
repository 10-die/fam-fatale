# Loading

**Built from humanity's absorption of the laser storm. Arc 2.**

## Origin

- Humanity absorbs the laser storm. That leads to *"the process that creates loading,"* and to **Winter** (see [../world/technology.md](../world/technology.md)).
- Trajectory: Loading → *"a machine human built out of material that charges from damage."*
- Has **artificial synapses** (a parallel to the rad's radioactive synapses).

## Kill switch

- Early on, **Loading gives King a remote with a button that shuts Loading down.** Pressing it makes Loading run an internal process that **reactivates his limitations.**

## Weapons

- **Finger gun.** He points his finger like a human pointing a gun, and it fires a **round of damage energy**. In effect, it fires a punch.
- He also uses it **for movement**, to displace himself and others.

## The Wings

- **Four wings** that **magnetically attach an inch off his back**, steered magnetically through his artificial synapses.
- **Stored in space, deployed from space.** When something's happening and Loading calls them, **you can hear them across the planet: a roar.**
  - Inside: *like several nuclear explosions back to back.* Not one-to-one, but cascading into each other as a steady stream. A jet **kilometers wide.**
  - In seconds they reach the altitude where that engine switches off, then shift to **jet-blast mode** and slowly drift down to the ground.
- Loading puts them on and **jet-blasts up.**
  - **Jet-blast mode is slower but omnidirectional**: forward, stop, back, left, right, up a bit.
- **Limited fuel**, in both the wings and the jet blasts.
- **Not a casual ultimate.** He has to *think* about when to call them. **Calling them too early or too late** is a real failure mode, and he **researches it.**

- **Mary Sue:** might ask him to recover her previous clones' memories.
