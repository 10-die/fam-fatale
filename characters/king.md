# King

See OPEN_QUESTIONS.md for the unresolved question of King's scope (national office vs. supra-national figurehead).

## Identity
- Leader of humanity, appointed by the ancient robots (the "custodians of humanity" — see engine/timeline.md) as leader of "the group," and humanity in turn chooses that group as their leaders "because it works" / because they organize things.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- This dump refers to him as "King of humans," and separately describes "the king of humans... just roaming on a planet somewhere, every country has its own stable government" — distinct from a specific national head of state.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9, ¶1
- Distinct from "the president of america Sten menli," who "flies around America in the Great bald eagle" — named separately and given a distinct vehicle/vibe, suggesting King and the US president are two different offices in this dump (contributes to, but doesn't fully resolve, the open King-scope question).
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Cannot personally order an abduction from the White House the way Winter can — Winter helped King build the White House specifically so it retains the capacity to contain *anything*, including "a rogue Loading." King is bound by that design; Winter isn't.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
- Doesn't need to be physically present for major arc-1 decisions — e.g. the room where Winter's terminal is rebooted proceeds without him.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
