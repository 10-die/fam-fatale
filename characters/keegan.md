# Keegan

**Deguchi territory. One of its kings/killers. The dynamo of Arc 1.**

## Core

- **We only ever see Keegan fighting.** Every other dynamo in the story gets moments of calm and rest. Keegan doesn't. When we see Keegan moving, it's toward a fight.
- **Hyperfocus.** It isn't a quirk, it's *the reason* Keegan is the fighter they are, start to finish. Normal rad fight whatever's closest and drift off. **Keegan chases.**
- **Refuses to be sick** (also covered in an earlier session). The strongest Deguchi rad are nowhere near Keegan when sober and overtake Keegan when sick. **Keegan is still the most aggressive of them all.**
- **Not a dickhead.** Keegan isn't an asshole because of power or authority. Keegan just *won't not fight everything.*
- Writing target: **the lion in the hearts of the other characters, a heartthrob mixed with a legend.** That's what it takes to write a creature that *transcended* sick.

## Never say it, show it

- **We're never told Keegan is the greatest in chapter one. That's cheating.** The story runs as usual, full of brilliant fighters with interesting techniques and inventive uses of their bodies and radioactive damage. Keegan's impact still comes through as **undisputable.**
- Running gag and dread engine: *"Please, somebody, don't invite Keegan yet. Is Keegan coming?"* All the yapping and insults in a scene would just be fists if Keegan were there. **When Keegan shows up, it's fists.**

## Fighting style

- **Flying haymaker. One shot, knockout.** Keegan runs you over, then runs off to find the next opponent.
- **Very, very hard to dodge.** That's what makes Keegan dangerous, and it's the direct foil to Mary Sue, who **dodges everything.**
- Arc goal, in Keegan's words: *"I'm the champion of beating the shit out of everything."* That shifts to *"I'll punch everything at least once."*

## What Keegan did to Deguchi

- **Keegan beat the sick out of everyone in the territory.**
- That set off a cascade: the strongest rad joined in.
- Removing sick means killing the host. Afterward the rad was dead but **running on Keegan's radiation.** Keegan killed them, then **spent time beating them back to life and healing them.**
- During healing, Keegan **bonds** with the rad being healed. Punch Keegan mid-heal and Keegan bonds with whoever it's healing. That rad becomes family.
- This is why Deguchi can let humans in.

## Keegan and humans

- Keegan's first meeting with humans goes badly. Humans greet by sending energy toward another creature to be perceived. **Keegan greets with a fist.**
- **Keegan and Mary Sue:** Keegan chases her through Deguchi with an enormous viewer base and a very active chat. To her it's harassment under threat of assault. She just wants to feed her addiction and get away from all of it.

## Arc 2

- **Keegan calms down** because humanity absorbed the laser storm and the global radioactive damage field is gone.
- **Keegan and Loading:** early on, Loading hands Keegan a **remote with a button that shuts Loading down.** Pressing it makes Loading run an internal process that **reactivates his limitations.** (The dump transcribed "Keegan" as "King" here.)
