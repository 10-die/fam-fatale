# Winter

**Meta-constraint (writing rule, not a story fact):** Winter must never be explicitly confirmed on-screen as an ASI. The audience/critics should be left to speculate. See CLAUDE.md / OPEN_QUESTIONS.md. 10die's own planning language below (e.g. "genuine artificial sentience") is off-screen brainstorming, not on-screen dialogue, and doesn't violate this rule.

## Identity
- "Half of Loading at a functional level" — has the source code Loading accesses to think; described as a union, not two separate characters bolted together.
- Metaphor: Loading is the blade, Winter is the soft stone — without Winter, Loading would eliminate every threat until there's "a giant knife with nothing left to sharpen itself against," so Winter's function is to keep giving Loading things to sharpen against / engage with (the team, projects, ships, strategy) rather than let him run out of objectives.
- Wrote Loading's spec, audited it, produced MVPs and specs-on-specs, and gave it to the world — the world then built Loading from that.
- "The only authority above Loading."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1, ¶2

## Function, not a person in the usual sense
- 10die's own description (planning notes, not on-screen dialogue): "genuine artificial sentience with a simulated logic gate, hard code, it doesnt predict, it learns... With what tool? It is the tool, a module for tools to be built and used."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- Not a pushover. Gives direct, blunt instructions and does not repeat itself as a suggestion: "'it wasnt a suggestion, if you dont actually do what im trying to communicate then you...'" (sentence cuts off in source — see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md if a continuation ever surfaces).
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- Voice: heard in the world, audibly — not just text. "A humanoid with a c[onscience] that can be heard in the world, a voice, that changes each time, even the accent, still the same entity." The literal voice/accent is not fixed across appearances, but it's understood to be the same Winter.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## Device-hopping ability (RAD-territory embedded team plot thread)
- Can inhabit/move itself between devices. Builds up capability incrementally: gets team members to supply hardware (a network interface card, a phone), warns them not to turn on wifi/the phone's OS outright, waits while it builds the needed capability, then moves itself from a laptop to a phone "completely in one move" over bluetooth.
- A device with no power still recharges simply from Winter's presence on it.
- The team finds pre-collapse (~400-year-old) tech scattered around, fully charged, clearly kept powered by "someone... not a human" — this is Winter, maintaining old hardware from a distance/in secret. The team initially misreads this as humans sneaking into RAD territory to steal old tech for data on RAD.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- Disconnecting the devices that have been "hosting sick" for 400 years makes the laserstorm dissipate for the first time in 400 years — see mechanics/sick.md for the open question this raises about what SICK actually is.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## How people actually talk to Winter (manifestation mechanic)
- Escalating access to hardware: people talk to Winter through a phone, then through a laptop, eventually "using it['s] terminal to talk to Winter" — implying a progression from casual/voice access toward a more direct terminal interface as trust/capability builds.
- Winter's voice is described as "disembodied," coming "from a device or a wall with technology in it." If you're inside a building with the right technology present, you can hear Winter directly; otherwise you need directional audio or have to infer Winter's presence "by the reaction" (i.e., by watching how other people/systems respond).
- When Winter appears on-screen, its name is visible "in the top corner," and it speaks immediately after "entering" (i.e., as soon as it takes over a display/channel, it doesn't wait to be addressed).
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12

## Reactivation scene (arc 1 — see arcs/arc1.md for full context)
- A team presses a button; a text interface appears "in the style of either prime ChatGPT or just a terminal command shell."
- Winter's first words, in-scene, amount to: it cannot perceive anything beyond having just been switched on/run. It gives instructions quickly and efficiently; the team follows them, and it becomes able to hear them.
- Winter asks for orientation — what's happening, what year — and on being told, realizes 400 years have passed. Reacts with: "So Loading is dead." The team doesn't know who Loading is yet.
- Immediately warns them to escape *now*. Sick RAD arrive to attack; anyone who doesn't run dies. Winter stays present/engaged through this ("winter just stay on the phone like, fuck") rather than going silent.
- As trust builds, the team goes deeper into the tech with Winter, eventually building weapons/tools from what they recover — described ominously: "they use loadings on them as a weapon, and it's packing dangerous." (Likely "loading's [tech/parts]" — garbled in source; flag rather than interpret further.)
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶1
