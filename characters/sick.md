# Sick (as a character, and as Keegan's sibling)

This file covers Sick as a *character*: a someone, and in one version Keegan's sibling. What sick is and how it works (spread, hosting, corpses, the kill transfer, the naming taboo) lives in mechanics/sick.md, and rebirth in mechanics/death-and-rebirth.md. Neither is restated here.

10die uses "Sick" both for the condition ("they're not sick") and for an actor that does things ("Sick does a jump kick," "Sick gets to watch"). Which is meant isn't always clear. See canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.

## Character traits
- **Always there.** "Sick is... Genuinely always there. If not in presence."
  source: raw/2026-09-27-keegan-mary-sue-combat.md, msg 1
- **Aware of Loading, and watching.** Loading and Sick "interact as if they've already been talking to each other, like they're on open comms." Faced with every human moving against it at once, "Sick does not get to win. Sick gets to watch as the inevitability" arrives, "wondering how this is happening." See characters/loading.md.
  source: raw/2026-09-27-keegan-mary-sue-combat.md, msg 1
- **Cruel.** "Maybe sick is also cruel. Maybe sick says the most heinous shit that a character could say because they're controlling the character... They know they have their memories. That is sick."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Maybe sick is also cruel")
- **A manipulator that treats everything as an object.** Sick got people to "constantly blame one another because there was actual evidence for it because they were being manipulated against one another," "planting the seeds for fighting to feed it." "But sick is evil. Sick uses others. Sick is itself an object that views everything as an object."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Its want.** "Sick wanted to spread that everywhere all at once forever."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
