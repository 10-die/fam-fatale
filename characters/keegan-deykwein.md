## Behavioral characterization — interaction and response patterns
- Keegan's behavior is response-dependent: he can go hostile to docile based on what he's encountering.
- When Keegan sees something interesting, he starts throwing punches. His dialogue is sparse — he responds with his fists and communicates through damage.
- He will keep hitting until Loading intervenes to stop him. Over time, through exhausting 40-minute forced fights, Keegan learns not to terrorize others.
- First genuine smile and cool composure: When Keegan grabs Loading's ulna and radius after Loading reassembles, Keegan flies toward Loading "with his first genuine smile on his face, like, genuine cool, like, not trying to be cool." This marks a shift in his demeanor — he's finally engaging with an opponent (Loading) that matches his intensity.
- Addiction to fighting: Keegan's addiction to fighting borders on pathological. He was taught by Mary Sue about running, losing, and not always fighting — concepts he despises most but learns to integrate.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Origin — Gucci territory
- Comes from Gucci territory (see forces/rad-territories.md), where SICK is not as normalized/"privileged" as it is in McConnell territory. Gucci's own leaders still have to go SICK sometimes, because they have to fight McConnell territory's leaders.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶12
- This lines up with the earlier "DaGucci College" location (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) from the Kyle/Mary Sue hallway scene — plausibly the same territory, though 10die hasn't explicitly connected the two.
- Keegan is from the strongest family in the Gucci kingdom.
- Gucci has centuries of fighting culture. Fighters had to become tougher and eventually got "stuck-tough" — stoic like lions but will actually engage and fight lions. Power comes not from RAD innate ability but from dedication to constant evolution, learning, and experimentation.
- Radiation boost is available to the strongest fighters in Gucci, a reward for their commitment to the culture.
- Keegan had lost joy in fighting because he was constantly fighting SICK — from McConnell's most infected RAD, from other territories' most infected RAD — just to keep Gucci sovereign and independent.
- Keegan had to sacrifice and get SICK himself when fighting other territory leaders, a necessary evil to maintain Gucci's position.
- Other territories cannot influence Gucci fighters to go SICK because Gucci's strongest aren't influenced by outside pressure — they resist based on internal conviction.
- McConnell invasion scenario: A direct McConnell invasion would eventually force Gucci into SICK exposure. Gucci has held them off through superior tactics and fighter quality, not through SICK resistance.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

## Mary Sue as teacher
- Mary Sue embodies what Keegan despises most: running from fate, being afraid to fight, losing, and not always fighting.
- Keegan learns from Mary Sue by being forced to engage with her through long exhausting fights. He almost forces her to learn the fighting lesson (to always fight, never run) but doesn't — he respects her limits.
- Mary Sue teaches Keegan to value these "despised" behaviors as legitimate survival and wisdom, not cowardice.
- In return, Keegan gives Mary Sue inhalants/vaping that produce an inebriated effect — "breath of fresh air with every strike you land, surge of dopamine and euphoria, keep going." This chemical support keeps her functional during intense extended combat situations.
- Their relationship is mutually transformative: Keegan learns when *not* to fight; Mary Sue learns to survive fighting without always fleeing.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18
