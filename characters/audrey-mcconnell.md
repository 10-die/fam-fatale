# Audrey McConnell

McConnell territory. The strongest McConnell, and where Arc 1 reaches its fever pitch. For the territory itself (the sitcom-style outskirts, the killing at the center, the sky vortex), see forces/rad-territories.md. For how sick works, see mechanics/sick.md.

10die uses "he" and "himself" for Audrey.

- Arc 1 peaks in McConnell territory, "when we meet Audrey McConnell." Mary Sue's addiction is what takes the story there.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("we meet Audrey McConnell")
- "The McConnells are so sick that Audrey McConnell beat himself into every McConnell. They all look like McConnell siblings. There's very little variation in the facial structure. Complete variation in race, gender."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("beat himself into every McConnell")
- "But they're not a hive, they're individuals. They're just the same individual. And they can send radioactive data to one another. But they prefer to just kill one another."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("they're not a hive")
- "Audrey McConnell dies all the time, but Audrey McConnell kills a lot. He has one of the higher deaths, death counters, with amongst the higher McConnell. And yet, he reigns supreme, perpetually."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("reigns supreme, perpetually")
