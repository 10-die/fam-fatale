# Audrey McConnell

**McConnell territory. The strongest McConnell. The fever pitch of Arc 1.**

- **So sick that he beat himself into every McConnell.** They all look like his siblings: very little variation in face structure, complete variation in race and gender.
- The McConnells **aren't a hive.** They're individuals who are all the same individual. They can send each other radioactive data, but **they'd rather kill each other.**
- **He dies all the time** and **kills a lot**, with one of the highest death counters among the top McConnells. **He still reigns supreme, perpetually.**
- **"The McConnell story is beautiful, and we're going to follow them."** Not villains. This story has no protagonist and no antagonist.

## The writing problem

> **How can every antagonist be as dangerous as the main antagonist?**

In McConnell territory there's no bottom tier. Everyone is at the top and equal, and Audrey is the strongest. Every McConnell has to read as lethal.
