# Story 2 — the Mars era (*Pseudocode* / *Bash Command*)

A **separate story**, set **centuries after** fam-fatale, on Mars. All new characters. Everything in this folder is Story 2 material only; nothing here is fam-fatale (Story 1) canon unless a file says so.

## What it is
- 10die's framing: the end of the first story sets up "a whole new story, all new characters, but you can see the impact of the previous I guess generation, the previous era, or you can see the trend at least."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Working title is undecided: "the story is called either pseudocode or bash command."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Setting: "Set centuries in the future. Where humanity has now colonized the surface of Mars. And there are cities under the ground." Humans use strange minerals, "and even when they speak, it's clear that these are strange humans."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- 10die on how it got here: the people of Earth "that survived the war against Sick," on a planet that is "destroyed and dying," move to Mars — "humans colonized Mars and they all went there."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("the population of Earth that survived the war against Sick")
- 10die's reaction when the idea landed: "it's so brilliant that I was shaking. It's setting up a whole new story."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Then the exodus:** "our planet's dying. We need to colonize Mars," told episode by episode (fallout, the world brainstorming the move, the first digging on Mars). That material belongs to the exodus arc on Earth — see [../arcs/arc3.md](../arcs/arc3.md). It is not duplicated here.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Story 2 starts centuries after that**, with Mars colonized.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

## How 10die wants to build it
- 10die is defining the world first and the story later, deliberately: "I give leeway to the story by saying, I'm going to define the world over time. And eventually, I'll get to interact with my memories as I write."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("I'm going to define the world over time")
- The ending comes first in 10die's head: "I think I know where I want them to end." (The ending itself isn't stated anywhere in the repo.) The hesitation about starting: "I don't want to start the story, this character did this. But how does that affect the final ending of the story?"
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("I think I know where I want them to end")
- What 10die wants out of it: "I love growth, to be able to follow someone as they grow."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("I love growth")
- The core conflict is still open. See [conflict.md](conflict.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
