## What it does
- **Energy resource.** "Strange metal is used for energy. It is a resource, because it essentially allows something with no actual engine to run, in the sense of actually doing things, just because of magnets."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("Strange metal is used for energy")
- **How it moves things.** "Change in magnetic flux moves this metal this way. The magnets shift the metal, and together shifts the structure."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("Change in magnetic flux")
- **Burned into the atmosphere.** Strange metal "gets mined and burned into the atmosphere and used for energy."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("burned into the atmosphere")
- **Where the electrons are — undecided.** "there's either electrons in the steak, or there's electrons in the atmosphere from all the strange metal that gets mined and burned into the atmosphere."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("either electrons in the steak")
- **Layered alloys.** The stick needs "a specific strange metal... but it has to be merged with other metals. So maybe the metals are like the resource in this world that have layers upon layers. We were like, how did somebody think of this?"
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("layers upon layers")
- Jars's jet is "made partially with Martian metal." See [characters/jars.md](characters/jars.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("made partially with Martian metal")

## The old-tech vs. new-tech split
- Because the metals don't behave the way existing tech expects, humanity split:
  - **Old tech:** "some humans stuck with the old technology, because when our old electromagnetic technology, when it interacts with the metals, something interesting happens." What that "something interesting" is isn't said.
  - **New tech:** "Some of the humans started trying to build new technologies that transcended basic electromagnetic technology, digital technology that we have. And they did so through computers with AI. That's what they had at the time. Really good AI."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("some humans stuck with the old technology")

## The stick
- **Name:** "the wand, the staff, the stick. I prefer just the stick."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("I prefer just the stick")
- **2026-09-30 version:** "humans have use metal they, they sharpen the metal into like long sticks and they conduct other things with that strange metal magnetically."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Material:** "It's a specific strange metal that allows for this, but it has to be merged with other metals."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("a specific strange metal that allows for this")
- **Used like a conductor's baton.** The user has "a stick, like a conductor, and they start conducting while demanding, while chanting." They point or throw the stick toward the object, then strike or flick to where it should go, and it moves there. "So you're essentially just able to throw things with an electromagnetic pull towards the tip of the wand, the staff, the stick."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("like a conductor")
- **Voice plus gesture.** The user speaks what they want ("I want a plate," "medium rare," "diced," "180 degrees for an hour") and conducts. See the [kitchen scene](scenes.md#the-martian-kitchen).
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("conducting while demanding, while chanting")
- **Passive state.** Speaking alone doesn't act: "I want eggs. But they don't bring something yet, because they haven't used the wand. So they have that wand in the passive state."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("that wand in the passive state")
- **Finesse.** "the speed of it can break your plate. If you do it too fast, you can break your plate. So you need to have a finesse to flick the wrist, because you're interacting with an electromagnetic field. You're steering an electromagnetic field. That's what you're doing in essence." A clean stop is the skill: the plate "stops with the wrist stop. And there's a clang, the plate hit the metal."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("You're steering an electromagnetic field")
- What the stick does to **heat** (the steak goes medium rare on command; "180 degrees for an hour" in the oven) isn't explained. Logged in [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("they say medium rare")
