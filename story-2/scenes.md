# Story 2 — Scene seeds

## The Martian kitchen
What it's for, in 10die's words: "What I was trying to do was capture the idea of the Martian."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("capture the idea of the Martian")

Transcription note: the dump writes "stake" for **steak**, and the first demand comes through as "I want a stick," which in context ("wherever that stake is, it moves") most likely also means steak. Not certain; see [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

Beats, in the order 10die gives them:
1. "a Martian storming into their home and yelling, and like they have a stick, like a conductor, and they start conducting while demanding, while chanting."
2. "I want a stick [steak]." "they start throwing their wand in the direction where their stick is, and they throw back to right in front of them. And wherever that stake is, it moves from where it is to directly in front of him."
3. "the person laughs, I want a plate, and they point at where the plates are, and so they flick towards the plates and flick under the stake, and they scoop it, and the plate flies out from wherever it is, and then like with the flick of a wrist, the plate swoops under the stake and stops with the wrist stop. And there's a clang, the plate hit the metal."
4. "they go to the stove, they realize they need a pan, they conduct towards where the pan is, and then strike the point where they want the pan to be, and the pan flies towards and ends up where that strike is."
5. The rule, stated mid-scene: "the speed of it can break your plate. If you do it too fast, you can break your plate. So you need to have a finesse to flick the wrist."
6. Pan with the steak on the stove: "I want butter. And they fling butter into the pan, and they say medium rare, and the steak is just medium rare and it's smoking, and they take it off the grill and they start a 5-minute timer."
7. "I want eggs. But they don't bring something yet, because they haven't used the wand. So they have that wand in the passive state."
8. "I want potatoes grilled, cheesy sauce all over them. And then they say, potatoes, bang! Potatoes come in to a pan. Some other carrots."
9. "they go to a chopping board, and they look towards a knife, and they fling at the knife, and they say, diced. And the knife comes in and dices, and then it's thrown back to where it needs to be."
10. Recap version: "throw the potatoes, the cream sauce, like do the dicing thing, throw it in the oven, and they say 180 degrees for an hour, or however long it takes to make grilled potatoes. And they throw it back out, and it's made, and it's finished, and it's cooked." (The sauce is "cheesy sauce" in beat 8 and "cream sauce" in the recap.)
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("a Martian storming into their home")

10die's reaction to it: "That's what I envisioned. Oh my goodness." And the open question it left: "What do I do with this idea?"
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("What do I do with this idea?")

Mechanics this scene shows are filed in [strange-metal.md](strange-metal.md#the-stick).

## Jars comes to Earth
Mars era: Jars travels from Mars to Earth. Originally imagined as fam-fatale arc 2 (2026-10-11), then moved to this story (2026-10-14). Why he goes, and who he fights there, are open (see [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)).
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("jars from the Martian colony of humans travels to Earth"); raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("the way that I envisioned Jars getting to Earth")

Beats, as 10die describes them:
1. **Framing.** "wide scale, like wide enough to process a Godzilla fight, but it's not Godzilla, it's humans, and they're kind of small, but you can see them, and you see them all, it's like a helicopter view."
2. Jars "flies the jet made partially with Martian metal and slides in and bounces and then shoots backwards and slingshots forwards. Literally not stopping momentum but changing its direction and shooting itself at one of the enemy who are flying or in the air for some reason, or a structure or a vehicle."
3. "the enemy try to dodge and Jarvis is able to maneuver the plane to swing around on a dime, swinging downwards, counteracting the dodge and striking that enemy into the ground."
4. "The enemy bounces off of the ground only to be struck again by Jaws's plane."
5. **Cockpit cam.** "you can see Jaws's body shaking and moving. He's throwing his body, he's switching dials, he's pulling levers, he's not just holding a steering wheel, he's like tweaking the behavior of the plane."
6. **Contrast.** Other "human or rad pilots or flying contraptions" slow down, become predictable, "or even stop sometimes and hover. Jaws never stops moving."
7. **The building.** "the plane is actually able to crash into a building and then bounce off of it while the building behaves as if the plane actually crashed through it."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("wide enough to process a Godzilla fight")

The raw mentions "rad pilots" among the other flyers. Whether rad exist in the Mars era is not stated anywhere else; logged in [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("other human or rad pilots")
