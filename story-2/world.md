# Story 2 — World (Mars)

The setting of Story 2. Material about the exodus itself (Earth's fallout, the world brainstorming the move) lives in [../arcs/arc3.md](../arcs/arc3.md); it's only referenced here where it sets up the Mars era.

## Mars, centuries later
- Humanity "has now colonized the surface of Mars. And there are cities under the ground."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Living under the surface traces back to the exodus-era worry about whether plants can grow on Mars: "If we can't just somehow dig to the core where for some reason there is fertility in the soil, live in the crust, under the surface. Something. And we will perish having tried."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- The people are **strange humans**: "humans use strange minerals. They interact with them in strange ways. And even when they speak, it's clear that these are strange humans." What makes their speech strange isn't said (see [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Who went: the population of Earth that survived the war against Sick, from a planet that is "destroyed and dying": "humans colonized Mars and they all went there."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("they all went there")

## Animals
- 10die's earlier idea was that animals left behind on Earth "just ended up evolving into something." 10die immediately pushed back on it: "King wouldn't make sense as a character if they didn't consider animals. Bringing animals over to Mars. Or at least trying." Not settled.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

## The Moon colony and slingshot public transit
- There's "a colony on the moon. And you can travel from Mars to the moon. That's just another thick place where they live." ("thick place" as transcribed; likely a dictation slip.)
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **The Moon as a station / slingshot.** The look: "you'll see things orbiting the moon like there's nothing orbiting and then all of a sudden you'll see some vehicles orbiting orbiting orbiting and then through some sort of chain reaction They will speed up and speed up... And then they're flung. And then they start to heat up through the air as they slow down. And they're glowing as they shoot, as they jet."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- The idea behind it (from the exodus-era brainstorm): "up until this point, we built things that try to push themselves through space instead of things trying to fling themselves through space and then slow down their approach." Crew safety at those speeds: "Maybe medically induced coma?" Travel time floated as "a day... Maybe, maybe a month, but a day, maybe a month, maybe a week. We'll figure it out."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **The heavy ships.** "Heavy, heavy, heavy, heavy, heavy. Overbearingly heavy shuttles and ships and they're thrown through space like projectiles they're essentially catapult they're slingshot." They curve with the destination's orbit, slow, crawl into lower orbit, "and slide lands down the side of a mountain where it's cooled off before joining transit."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Revision in the same message** ("Oh, actually, wait, I had another idea"): the "super, super, super, super, super heavy, like almost tungsten laden ships" are the slingshots, "and from them emerge ships that can actually land and maneuver."
  - There are **space stations in orbit above Mars**.
  - The smaller craft "look like the, the passenger airlines we have now, but souped up. Like, Like, cool, like Star Wars-ish. Not just tubes, but like, it's got some flair to it. Like the Concorde would be a style that's used."
  - The heavy ships "run like one and then another one and then another one like trains. Like this is public transit by the way."
  - Whether the revision replaces the slide-landing version or both exist is logged in [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

## The drills
Two versions, from two sessions. Kept side by side; how they relate is open (see [../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)).

- **The air drill (2026-09-30).** In the exodus-era episode on Mars: "them on the surface of Mars, literally digging, a digging operation to dig down, down, down, down, down, and then branch out. And the most important thing to manage is the system that is getting the, the whatever is under the ground out."
  - "It's like a huge drill. It's a drill made of air. Powered by thermonuclear magnets. Maybe it's powered by ships, the ships that humans use, thermonuclear reactors. And it's actually two ships spiraling, but not moving. And instead, their thrust, which is like rips of solid wind, rip through the air and dig dirt out of a giant hole like... A kilometer, maybe five."
  - "humanity is all watching this operation that they're all invested in."
  - Samples from the dig are caught and tested — this is where strange minerals are first found. See [strange-metal.md](strange-metal.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **State-sized drills (2026-10-14).** The survivors "use their insane architectural skills to build massive drills, state-sized drills, and start mining and terraforming. And they start finding deposits of metals."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("state-sized drills")

## Terraforming and survival
- 2026-10-14: after arriving, they start "mining and terraforming."
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("start mining and terraforming")
- The exodus-era version of the problem (see [../arcs/arc3.md](../arcs/arc3.md) for the full brainstorm): "We get there, we can't breathe. We need to build structures that we can live in first... Like trying to live under the water." "We need an atmosphere. How long will it take to create an atmosphere?" "We need to experiment on whether we can actually grow plant life on Mars. We don't have time to build an atmosphere before we get there, but we have time to build hubs where we can actually survive." "Everything that ends up happening going forward is in service of that goal."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Strange metal is "mined and burned into the atmosphere and used for energy." Whether that burning is part of terraforming is never said. See [strange-metal.md](strange-metal.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("burned into the atmosphere")

## Society
- **Exodus-era baseline** (carried in from the move; detail in [../arcs/arc3.md](../arcs/arc3.md)): organizations "would need to be clear and concise and simple. Simple break, breakdowns of complicated and complex and intricate things. The, the average human is incredibly educated and inc- incredibly capable of Critical thinking. Efficient. And their society is fair. As fair as possible."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Mars era: meritocratic.** Not inherited wealth: "it wouldn't be based off of some yes I was rich and I was fancy and I was born and, and now I just get nice things I get a castle I get the castle and the horse." Instead: "it's meritocratic. The best get the best."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- It risks tribalism: "becomes very tribal there is a standard." The likely friction: people competing "inevitably over resources over the best places to be." Read alongside 10die's refusal to have humans wipe each other out — see [conflict.md](conflict.md).
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- Humanity got this far only by working together: "the only way humanity got this far is by be- being together. Working together."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- The tech split (old-tech vs. new-tech humans) is a division inside Mars society. See [strange-metal.md](strange-metal.md#the-old-tech-vs-new-tech-split).
  source: raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md ("some humans stuck with the old technology")
