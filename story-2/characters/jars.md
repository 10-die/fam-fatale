## Core
- "Jars is a Martian pilot, fighter pilot."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("Jars is a Martian pilot, fighter pilot")
- **He never stops moving.** "Jaws never stops moving, just glides around, bouncing his plane through the air." Other "human or rad pilots or flying contraptions" are different "because those will eventually slow down and become predictable in movement or even stop sometimes and hover." His piloting is what "makes him distinct."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("Jaws never stops moving")
- **Momentum is redirected, never lost.** The jet "slides in and bounces and then shoots backwards and slingshots forwards. Literally not stopping momentum but changing its direction and shooting itself at one of the enemy."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("Literally not stopping momentum")
- **He rides magnetic currents.** "Mastering manipulating the magnetic currents that are just in the atmosphere as a result of the end of the first arc." (Written while 10die still placed Jars in fam-fatale. Where those currents come from in the Mars era is open; see [../../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../../canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).)
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("magnetic currents that are just in the atmosphere")

## The plane
- "the jet made partially with Martian metal."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("made partially with Martian metal")
- **No weapons. He rams.** "Other planes have bombs and guns and knives and things that are thrown and dropped. Jaws just rams the front of the plane into something."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("Jaws just rams the front of the plane")
- **The nose takes nothing, the target takes everything.** "the focal point, the design of the plane, it takes almost no damage, and all of that damage is imparted on the target. So the plane is actually able to crash into a building and then bounce off of it while the building behaves as if the plane actually crashed through it, using the plane as the weapon."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("using the plane as the weapon")
- 10die on this: "that's the type of thinking I want in this story, in this show, in whatever it ends up being."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("that's the type of thinking I want")

## How he fights
- He fires himself "at one of the enemy who are flying or in the air for some reason, or a structure or a vehicle."
- "the enemy try to dodge and Jarvis is able to maneuver the plane to swing around on a dime, swinging downwards, counteracting the dodge and striking that enemy into the ground. The enemy bounces off of the ground only to be struck again by Jaws's plane."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("swing around on a dime")

## In the cockpit
- "there's a cam inside and you can see Jaws's body shaking and moving. He's throwing his body, he's switching dials, he's pulling levers, he's not just holding a steering wheel, he's like tweaking the behavior of the plane, and its momentum is all but guaranteed."
  source: raw/2026-10-11-jars-martian-pilot.md ("he's not just holding a steering wheel")
