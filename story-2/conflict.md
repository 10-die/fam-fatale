## 10die's constraints
- **Sick is gone.** "if I want to commit to sick being gone I would need to create some sort of replacement for the thing that's the tension." 10die's wording is "if I want to commit," so this is a strong lean, not a locked rule.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **No humans exterminating each other.** "I really don't want them to fight one another. Because where does that lead? If if you push them to the brink of the, of the asymptotal of effort towards that goal. They'll exterminate one another efficiently and well. And it will be an interesting fight. But then they're gone. Is that how I want a story to end? Because I follow the entire story to, for it to just reach the end and snuff itself out? No."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Some friction is expected.** People would fight "each other inevitably over resources over the best places to be."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **Meritocratic, not inherited.** Not "I was rich and I was fancy and I was born and, and now I just get nice things I get a castle I get the castle and the horse." Instead: "it's meritocratic. The best get the best." It "becomes very tribal there is a standard."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **It needs a direction.** The story has to answer "what is being worked toward?" and why someone would watch the next episode.
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1

## Assistant suggestions (unconfirmed — not 10die's canon)
The assistant offered five options in the 2026-09-30 session. 10die hasn't responded to any of them in the raw. Each one is an **(assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)**.
  source (all of this section): archive/sessions/2026-09-30-mars-colonization/arc-3-and-bash-command.md ("Conflict options for Story 2 (suggestions)")

1. **The title is the conflict: Pseudocode vs. Bash.** Pseudocode is intent (readable, forgiving, not executable); bash executes exactly what's typed, not what's meant. The strange metal does precisely what's commanded, with no malice and no mercy — it's an object, the way Sick was "an object that views everything as an object," and it makes it easy for people to treat each other as objects (users, permissions, processes to `kill`). Each episode, someone's intent and command diverge. Same idea as Story 1, power that feels like guaranteed victory, but with no villain to blame. (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)
2. **`sudo`: who has root?** In a meritocracy the question is "who has permissions?" Fluency with the metal is uneven — maybe Mars-born kids speak it natively (which is why they sound strange) and older lineages learned it second. A quiet schism of guilds or tribes defined by which commands they're trusted to run: is aptitude a fair basis for power when aptitude changes what's physically possible? (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)
3. **The echo of respawn.** Someone finds the metal might allow an `undo` / restore — Sick's offer (finality, then respawn) with no Sick attached. A society founded on refusing Sick has to decide whether to take it back: should we be able to come back? (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)
4. **Mars isn't finished with them.** Why is the metal strange, and why so deep? Maybe Mars had its own history that ended the way Earth nearly did, and the strange minerals are what's left. The deeper they dig, the more they learn, and whether it's happening again. Pairs with the "both planets lost their magnetic field" rhyme. (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)
5. **Earth is still up there.** The debris ring, the dying field, whatever stayed behind (animals, holdouts, Sick's remains). "Sick is gone" stays true while its scar remains: does anyone go back? Should they? (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)

**The assistant's recommendation:** 1 + 2 as the spine, 3 as the long-arc temptation, 4 as the mystery. (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)

Later archive notes (2026-10-10 session) tie two of these to 10die's newer material: the tech split as the fault line for option 2, and Jars's trip to Earth as a fit for option 5. Those links are also the assistant's. (assistant suggestion, unconfirmed)
