# Humans

## Founding principle

Humanity blames the rad for sick. In response it banded together and said: **fuck the old order.** The new society would use humanity's brilliance to build institutions that keep it safe.

**Every human must:**
- be able to fight to the death
- be trained, fed, and housed
- be rehabilitated if they survive
- be ready to defend the clones of the humans who died

> Humans exist because they have to **fight to live.** Rad **live to fight.**

## Temperament

- **Logical and not insecure.** They don't shy away from taboo topics in general. That's what makes the few real taboos (see *sick children* below) hit hard.
- **Pleasant and approachable.** They crack jokes and tease. They push past the fear of breaking social tension the wrong way. *"Hey, can I talk to you about this?" "Sure, what do you want to know?"* Even when they're busy: *"talk to this guy, here's his stream, he knows about it, I gotta go."*
- Writing them as genuinely human is what makes them likable.
- **They don't trust rad one iota.** When they look at a rad, they see sick. Humans have intellectualized their existential conflict with the rad territories and tie them closely to sick. Many carry horrible memories of their own run-ins with sick.

## The border

- Humans fight at the border wearing **reflective technology**. The environment holds so much radiation that it doesn't fully protect them.
- Over time they get **inundated with radiation** and have to back off. Otherwise they'd end up *"sleep fighting"*: the radiation pulls them toward rad behavior.
- Pulling people off the line and isolating them is the **best way to preserve humans without cloning them**, because the cloning system is always busy.
- **Losing a human to a rad territory happens.** This is a third outcome, separate from knockout and death. It's part of why the stigma is so strong.

### Isolation and sobriety

1. A human comes back from the border carrying radiation and goes into **isolation**.
2. They go through **awful, excruciating self-damage in vain.** You can't damage yourself and heal and get the joy of fighting or the radiation. You try anyway, your body hurts, and you recover.
3. Once their room reading is **zero**, they're removed and taken somewhere to recover. **They're sober now.**
4. Sober humans are then responsible for **raising the clones of dead humans** in the cores.

## Cloning (medical and scientific respawning)

- Every human's **DNA is already collected.**
- When a human dies, the DNA is used to make **a sperm and an egg.**
- A **giant incubation chamber** fuses them, triggering the natural process of a child forming. It's powered by radioactive energy focused from the tech that strips radiation out of human territory.
- By the time the dead person's team gets back from the border, **a four-year-old is waiting who looks exactly like the person they just watched die.**
- **Clones don't keep memories.** Two possible models (undecided, see [../story/open-questions.md](../story/open-questions.md)):
  - **A. Random family.** The clone goes to a random family and makes new memories. *Feels more authentic.*
  - **B. Same family.** The clone goes back to the same family, who remind it what it used to gravitate toward. *Easier for continuity, but feels stuck.*
- Culturally, family is **whoever you're with.** *I'm family with whoever I'm around, because we have to survive together.* You can be cut off from one family, join another group, and become their family. They take care of you and you take care of them.
- Working name for the system: **respawn protocol** / medical-scientific respawning.

## The cores

- Each human territory has **several cores** near its center.
- A core city is **houses, none taller than two or three stories.**
- At the center is the **largest building: an apartment block where every room connects to the others, so it's effectively one building.** This is the heart of human cloning.
- **Children are raised in the cores**, where the chance of sick is **0%**. Every adult there is trained to fight to the death. Sick would have to fight one human, then the next, then the next. *It's like shrinking yourself to the size of a bean and trying to get into a beehive.*

## Sick among humans

- Sick in humans is **a serious problem.** The exact reason it's so much worse in humans is still being worked out.
- **Sick children are taboo**, even to think about. A sick child knows it's too small to kill by fighting, so it **sets traps to execute adults** and makes them all sick. The adults then go back to acting normal, and now there are **sick spies.**
- **Investigations:** whenever sick is beaten out of someone and they're sent to isolation, there has to be an investigation into *who they last interacted with* and *whether those people are still alive.*
  - **Evidence of a death with no body means there's a sick.**
  - Maybe a corpse that no one has touched disappears after a certain time, or at night.
- See the scene seeds in [../story/scene-seeds.md](../story/scene-seeds.md): the corpse jumping out the window, and the second sick.

## Rebuilding

- **Humans build very fast** (especially after the [technology](technology.md) leap).
- **Department of Destruction:** when a city is ravaged by sick, or by an invasion from a bordering rad territory (sick or not), and humans reclaim it, **the whole Earth pours resources in to rebuild it as something new**, built by the people who'll live there.
- People who die in it are **respawned somewhere else.**
