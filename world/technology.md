# Technology

## Before: radiation-stripping tech

- Human territory runs tech that **removes radiation** from the environment.
- The energy it collects powers the **cloning incubators** (see [humans.md](humans.md#cloning-medical-and-scientific-respawning)).
- Border fighters wear **reflective tech**, which isn't enough to block everything.

## The big shift (between Arc 1 and Arc 2): absorbing the laser storm

- The **laser storm** is the planet-wide mesh of radiation that rad rebirth runs on (see [rad-and-sick.md](rad-and-sick.md#death-and-rebirth-the-red-cloud)).
- Humanity couldn't defeat the rad, because rad who die are born again. Instead, **humans figured out how to absorb the laser storm and use it.**
- **The global radioactive damage field goes down.** That's what calms Keegan in Arc 2.

## Winter and Loading

From the dump: *"absorbed by humans to create loading, to create the process that creates loading, to create winter."* How Loading and Winter relate is still loose (see [../story/open-questions.md](../story/open-questions.md)).

- **Winter** is a leap beyond LLMs and agents as big as **the first wheel compared with a modern Formula One wheel**: *what's the peak of a wheel in one specific application?* That's Winter.
- **The first thing Winter does is help humans orient themselves.** Winter reaches out and connects, and that's easy for it because humanity has built so much.
- **Loading** is a character (see [../characters/loading.md](../characters/loading.md)), possibly also the process that produces him. The endpoint is *"a machine human built out of material that charges from damage."*

## The electron layer (magnetic infrastructure)

- The absorbed rad energy becomes **a layer of oriented electrons in the atmosphere**, which computers can configure.
- **Make a magnetic field, set your laptop in it, and it stays there.** You can type on it, slide it along the field line, and it resists being pulled off that line.
- **Vehicles are magnets that can flip their polarity** and ride the electron layer.
- This is part of why humans build so fast in the Arc 2 era.
