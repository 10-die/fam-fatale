# Rad and Sick

## Rad

- **Rad live to fight.** They're having a blast, even in the territories that have gone sick. (Humans *fight to live*. See [humans.md](humans.md). This is the central contrast of the story.)
- Rad are **like deer**: if they're interacting with you, eventually they'll try to hit you. They want one thing: to hit and get hit for a while.
- **Healthy rad don't chase.** A rad you fought and escaped will follow you for a bit, then bump into another rad and start fighting that one instead. Rad fight whatever is closest.
  - Exception: **Keegan**, who hyperfocuses (see [../characters/keegan.md](../characters/keegan.md)).
- A rad's **greeting is a punch.** A human greets another creature by sending energy toward it so it can be perceived. A rad does that with its fist.

### Bonding (radioactive synapses)

- Rad **bond fast and part fast**: you're family for a moment, then you never see them again.
- While bonded, you pick up the other rad's **radioactive synapses**. They shape your mind a little and your mannerisms take after theirs for a while.
- Bonding happens through healing. When Keegan is beating a rad back to life, that rad becomes family. If you punch Keegan in the middle of that, Keegan bonds with whoever it's healing.
- **Radiation exposure works on humans too.** Humans fighting at the border slowly get *inundated* (see [humans.md](humans.md#the-border)).

### Death and rebirth (the red cloud)

- Death **emits radiation.**
- Where rad die often (especially in sick territories), the radiation builds into a **cloud of radioactive blood gas**.
- The cloud fires a **red laser beam** down at the biomass on the ground, and **the rad is reborn.**
- **Memory survives**, because the rad was in the cloud while it was dead.
- **The cloud is a dream / virtual server** hosted by all of that radiation. Inside it, rad can **see from the perspective of the rad (or human) they recently interacted with.**
  - The dump says *"that's where Mary Sue comes in"* (see [../characters/mary-sue.md](../characters/mary-sue.md)).
- Planet-wide, this shows up as the **laser storm**: a mesh of radiation over the whole planet. Humanity eventually absorbs it (see [technology.md](technology.md)).

## Sick

> Sick makes whatever it inhabits fixate on **how easy it would be to end the existence of another creature.**

- Sick lives in the gap between *the moment that thought shows up* and *standing over a corpse*.
- **How it spreads:** kill someone, beat the corpse *into* sick, then both run off in different directions to do it again.
- **To remove sick you have to kill the host.** Keegan does this, then spends the time to *beat them back to life* and heal them.
- **Sick prioritizes ease:** *I'll kill you, then you, then both of you at once.*
- **Sick has the host's memories.** It doesn't read them, it simply *has* them. That's why it can say the most heinous things that person could say. Sick is cruel, vicious, vile.
- **Sick is strategic, not cackling.** It doesn't go *"ha ha, I'm sick, you can't catch me."* **Sick executes.** It hides corpses, acts normal, and plants spies.
- **Sick makes rad stronger.** The strongest Deguchi rad are nowhere near Keegan when they're sober. When they're sick, they overtake Keegan.

### Sick territories

- In territories that have gone **completely sick**, the rad take sick as their *inspiration*. They want to be like sick.
- They kill each other constantly: *kill you, kill you, kill you. I can kill you.* That's how they talk to each other.
- All that death feeds the red cloud, so they keep getting reborn and the cycle keeps going.
- **The tone is not grim.** You'd expect a dark, dreary, mechanical sludge-fest of horrors. **No.** They're having *so much fun.* It's ridiculous.
