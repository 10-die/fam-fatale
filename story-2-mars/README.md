## Strange metal

> source: `notes/raw/2026-10-14-mars-strange-metal-and-the-stick.md` (Mars branch: "strange minerals / Martian minerals / strange metal")

- The deposits they find **don't behave the way our technology expects metals to behave.**
- **Strange metal is an energy resource.** It's mined and **burned into the atmosphere.** It lets things with **no engine** run, purely through magnets: *a change in magnetic flux moves this metal this way. The magnets shift the metal, and together they shift the structure.*
- The steering works either because there are **electrons in the object** or because there are **electrons in the atmosphere** from all that burned strange metal. Undecided.
- **Metals come in layers upon layers.** Useful materials are alloys of strange metal *merged with other metals*. The reaction to them should be *"how did somebody think of this?"*

## The tech split

- **Old-tech humans** stayed with familiar electromagnetic and digital technology, **because something interesting happens when old EM tech interacts with strange metal.** (What happens is still open.)
- **New-tech humans** built technology that **transcends electromagnetic and digital tech.** They did it **with AI**, the really good AI they had at the time. **That's how bash commands came about.**
  - This lines up with the Mars branch's *sudo / who has root* conflict option. The split could be the fault line.

## The stick

The everyday tool of the new tech. 10die prefers "stick" over "wand" or "staff."

- Made from **one specific strange metal merged with other metals.**
- **It conducts.** It's used like a conductor's baton. Point and flick at an object, then strike where you want it, and it **flies to that spot.** In effect, it throws things with an electromagnetic pull toward the stick's tip.
- **Voice plus gesture.** The user speaks the intent (*"I want a plate," "diced," "medium rare," "180 degrees for an hour"*), and the stick carries it out.
- **Passive state:** saying *"I want eggs"* with the stick idle does **nothing.** Nothing moves until the stick is used.
- **It takes finesse.** You're **steering an electromagnetic field.** Flick too fast and the plate arrives fast enough to **break.** A clean wrist-stop is the skill.
