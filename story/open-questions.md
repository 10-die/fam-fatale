# Open Questions

Things the dump raised but didn't settle, or that pull against each other.

## Sick

- **Why is sick so much worse in humans?** It has to be bad enough to scare logical, secure people.
- **Sick children:** the dump says children *can't* become sick (so they're raised in the cores), and also that sick children are taboo and set traps for adults. Possible fix: the cores guarantee 0% sick, but a sick child *outside* a core is the nightmare scenario, and that's why the taboo exists.
- **When does an untouched corpse disappear?** After a fixed time? At night?
- Can a **human** body be reborn by the red cloud, or only rad?

## Rebirth and memory

- **Clone placement: random family (A) or same family (B)?** A feels more authentic and fits Mary Sue wanting permanence. B is easier for continuity.
- **Lock in the rebirth rule?** Proposed: a kill before death means near-instant rebirth, no kill means batch rebirth from the cloud. That would reward killing, which helps explain why sick territories churn so fast. Does it apply to Deguchi rad too, where deaths are rarer?
- **What happens to rad rebirth after humanity absorbs the laser storm?** If the red cloud *is* the rebirth system, absorbing it could make death permanent for rad in Arc 2. That would be huge, and it might be part of why Keegan calms down.
- **How do Mary Sue's past clones' memories survive?** Through the red cloud's perspective-sharing ("that's where Mary Sue comes in")?

## Mary Sue

- **What exactly is she?** A human (her clones lose memories) who's addicted to radiation/fighting and keeps getting lost to rad territory? Or something in between?
- **What is she addicted to**, specifically: radiation, the rush of fighting, bonding?
- **What goes wrong** in her Arc 2 sobriety? ("Mary Sue turns lethal" is shelved.)
- Why do so many of her clones end up deep in McConnell territory?

## Keegan

- Pronouns/framing: the dump uses both "they" and "it." Pick one.
- Do Keegan and Mary Sue ever actually connect, or is the chase the relationship?
- **Why doesn't Keegan leave?** Every other healthy rad is satisfied after a few blows. Is Keegan's "never satisfied" the same thing as the hyperfocus, or a separate trait?
- If Keegan won't stop, how does Keegan **bond**? Does the brawl contract's bonding step even apply?

## Brawl contract

- Does nervous-system sharing go both ways? Can a **human** feel the rad's emotions too?
- What counts as "enough blows"? Is it a set number, or does it depend on the rad?

## Loading and Winter

- **How are they related?** Is Loading a product of Winter, the process that leads to Winter, or both? Is Loading the name of the tech *and* of the character?
- Who made Loading, and why did he give **King** his off switch?

## Story 2 (Mars) and Jars

- **Jars's name:** Jars, Jarvis, or Jaws? The transcription used all three. The in-depth version lives in claude.ai chats this repo can't see. Paste or export them into `notes/raw/` to reconcile.
- **Why does Jars come to Earth** in the Mars era? Who is he fighting there?
- **Drills:** the air drill (two thermonuclear ships spiraling, Mars branch) vs. **state-sized drills** (2026-10-14). Two stages, or competing versions?
- **What happens when old EM tech touches strange metal?** That's the reason some humans stuck with old tech.
- **Where are the electrons:** in the steered objects, or in the strange-metal-saturated atmosphere?
- **Is the Arc 1 electron layer the ancestor of Mars's strange-metal atmosphere?**
- **What does the stick actually do with voice?** Is the spoken word the "command" and the flick the "execute"? Or does the voice only *name* the target while the flick does everything?
- How do "medium rare" and "180 degrees for an hour" work? That's heat, not motion. Does the stick drive appliances, or does the metal heat things directly?
