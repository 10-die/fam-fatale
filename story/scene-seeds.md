# Scene Seeds

## Keegan flies a fighter jet

*(Framed as "if Keegan existed today and had to fight a dragon." It could be adapted or kept as an aside.)*

1. A **dragon** (the "Chorza dragon") is running amok. Keegan *runs away* from it.
2. **You hear a fighter jet.** Keegan is at the controls with a **knocked-out pilot sitting in Keegan's lap**, making a beeline for the dragon.
3. The pilot is unconscious but taking in Keegan's radiation, so **the limp body animates** and hits specific controls and switches, matching Keegan move for move.
4. **A missile fires.** The plane climbs, **rising while turning.**
5. **The pilot screams awake** and reaches for the eject handle. **Knocked out the moment their fingers touch it.** Springs back up, eyes closed again.
6. **From the dragon's side:** it's been hit in the arm or wing. Serious damage, it can't take off. It looks around and spots the plane, which looked like it was fleeing and is now far away.
7. The plane **circles the dragon, accelerating unsettlingly fast**, high up.
8. Near the half-circle mark it **turns its nose toward the dragon** and swoops in sideways, *like water circling a drain*. **It fires every gun and every missile and every shot lands.** Not an explosion here and there. *Everything.*
9. Just before reaching the dragon, **the nose tilts up and it drops a load of bombs** right on it.
10. Then it rips back into the sky.

> Keegan to the engineers: *"Make it so the plane doesn't fall apart slicing through the air sideways, so I can keep the target at my center and hit it with everything we've got. I know I could just be a show of force, but I prefer to be a weapon. My own weapon."*

### Physics notes (answering the question in the dump)

- **Projectiles inherit the plane's velocity.** If the nose points at the dragon but the plane is moving *sideways* (circling), that sideways speed carries into every round. **The rounds don't go faster toward the target. They drift sideways and would miss** unless the shooter aims ahead (leads) to compensate.
  - Rough scale: a cannon round at ~1,000 m/s from a plane sliding sideways at ~250 m/s drifts about 14° off the nose.
  - Speed only adds *toward* the target to the extent the plane is closing on it. The circling-the-drain spiral does close in, so there's *some* added punch.
- **So "every shot lands" is the Keegan part.** Keegan and the radiation-linked pilot are computing lead on the fly. That's the flex, and it's worth showing.
- **Missiles** steer themselves, so firing off-axis works. Real ones do lose energy turning hard after launch.
- **Nose-up bomb release is a real technique (toss / loft bombing).** Pulling up at release flings the bombs forward along an arc. Keegan's move is legit.
- **Flying nose-off-direction-of-travel** is real but brief: post-stall / thrust-vectoring maneuvers like Pugachev's Cobra, X-31, Su-57. Sustaining it is exactly what Keegan is demanding the engineers make possible.

## The corpse out the window

At night, a corpse **springs to life, jumps out the window, and vanishes into the dark.** Next episode, we see that character walking around as if nothing happened.

## The second sick

A sick-investigation scene with only a couple of people left. The audience knows who the sick is and is **rooting for the other one to find them.** Then **the other one attacks first. They were sick too.** *"Oh no, I thought they'd be the one to stop it."*

## Evidence, no body

Investigators retrace someone's last interactions. **There's evidence of a death but no body.** That's the moment everyone knows there's a sick.

## Return from the border

A team comes home to a core city: low houses and the giant connected apartment block in the center. **A four-year-old who looks exactly like the teammate they just watched die is already waiting.**

## Isolation

A border fighter alone in a room, trying to hurt themselves into the feeling of fighting. It doesn't work. They recover. They try again. Eventually the room reading hits zero and they walk out sober.

## Loading's wings

Something happens somewhere. **The whole planet hears a roar.** Seconds later, four wings are coming down out of space.
