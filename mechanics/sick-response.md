# The human response to sick

How humans detect, fight, investigate, and clean up after a sick outbreak inside human territory. What sick *is* and how it behaves belongs to mechanics/sick.md; this file is the human side. Contradictions are logged in canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.

## The stakes
- "Sick must be dealt with swiftly when within humanities boarders, that's how you lose a whole state to the RAD." "2 sick can equal a war 3 it's every human." "If that sick is not labotimised humanity must prepare to for a war."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶14
- "Sick starts and spreads up wild, like a wildfire and it's snuffed out. Humanity just collapses all of its resources on it. That's what the governments do, because the governments are everyone." See forces/human-society.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
- "When humans become sick, it's a problem, a serious problem. And I'm going to figure out why." 10die wants the reason to be strong enough to scare humans who "are not insecure" and "very logical." (Why sick is worse in humans is still open.)
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("when humans become sick, it's a problem")

- "The moment it becomes sick, an alarm rings in the entire human country. And it's gonna be seconds before there's a human killing agent there to land this first blow, hopefully saving lives."
- "And then agents start trickling in and flooding the place because the first agent is probably going to lose. But then there's four agents, and then there's 12 agents, and then it just cascades. To a flood of agents in an entire area, deployed rapidly, one at a time, spammed, spammed, spammed."
- Agents are already moving: "literally taking trans, have already been on transit." Trains "zip across the country"; your car breaks off and slows down "Till you get to, to a terminal of your destination in a shuttle. That is designed to absorb that energy of slowing down." See forces/human-society.md (Transit).

## Danger points: sleep, and children
- "Sometimes, if they're sleeping, that's the most dangerous time because then they can just become sick. And that's when the children need to escape." Children co-stream and "sometimes they have to fight back the adults." See mechanics/cloning-and-family.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-03-post-collapse-society.md, msg 1
- **Sick children (2026-10-04):** "when kids become sick, it's a problem because they lie. When adults become sick, they can't help it. They go in a frenzy. When children become sick, they become spies. Undetectable. Intentions undetectable. And then a moment of being alone together. Death. And now there's two of them, both pretending. No one notices a thing because there's no corpse, because the corpse is moving around."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- **Sick children (2026-10-10):** "it's still taboo to think about a sick child, because sick children understand they are too small to kill through damage, through fighting. So they set up traps to execute adults. They make them all sick. And the adults go back to normal. And now there are sick spies."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("taboo to think about a sick child")
- **But also (same 2026-10-10 dump):** "Children cannot become sick, so they are in this core." Logged as a contradiction in canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Children cannot become sick")
- **The cores** are kept at "a 0% chance of sick" because every adult there is trained to fight to the death ("trying to just get into a beehive"). See mechanics/cloning-and-family.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("0% chance of sick")

## After: isolation and the investigation
- "When sick is dealt with, when sick is beaten out of someone, and then they're taken to isolation, there's an investigation that must happen into who they last interacted with and are they still alive."
- **Evidence of death without a body:** "You'll see the evidence of death, but no body. Then you know there is a sick."
- **The vanishing corpse:** "maybe at night or something, or there's a certain amount of time before a corpse disappears with no one having ever interacted with it." 10die's scene: "at night and you see a corpse spring to life and jump out the window, disappear into the night. And in the next episode, we see that character."
- **The second sick:** in a scene with only a few people left, "the other one just attacks, and then the sick that we know attacks. It's like, oh no, I thought that they would be the one to prevent sick. I was rooting for them."
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("there's an investigation that must happen")
- "Sick will execute." It isn't "ha ha ha, look at me, I'm sick, you can't catch me." That's what makes investigations necessary. (Sick's behavior: mechanics/sick.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Sick will execute")
- Why the investigation comes after, never before: "A genuine threat that Once it happens, that's when it can be dealt with. Before it happens, it's speculation."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 1
- Isolation for border fighters (the room reading, sobriety) is in forces/humans.md.
