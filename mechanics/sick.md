- The strongest RAD, per this framing, "used to just be deadly and to be reborn quickly, like, even quick enough to respawn right before a territorial boundary war ends."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2

## The laserstorm / tech-hosted framing (new in this dump)
- Fighting, dying, and being reborn produces a "laserstorm" that releases radioactive damage — described as the thing that "built up over time" and as being "the story is at its core about."
- The laserstorm/SICK has apparently been "hosted" on scattered pre-collapse (~400-year-old) devices, kept powered in secret by Winter (see characters/winter.md, device-hopping section). When the embedded RAD-territory team disconnects those devices on Winter's direct order, the laserstorm dissipates "for the first time in 400 years."
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9

## Core definition — "SICK is a workflow" (major clarification)
- Explicit rejection of a zombie-virus comparison: "SICK is not a zombie virus, unfortunately, because zombie viruses would be easy to handle. Just don't let them bite you, and they'll shuffle around aimlessly — their only advantage being that there's so many of them, but they are not coordinated intentionally."
- The actual thesis, given close to verbatim because it's load-bearing: "This story is dark. SICK is not a zombie. SICK is a workflow. Think about it like that — 'cause if I were to become sick, I would still be me, but my objectives would always be done in the most [malicious] way possible." 10die's own example: a sick baseball pitcher would still just be pitching, but every pitch becomes a deliberate attempt to injure the batter — aim for the neck because he keeps dropping his shoulder, intentionally. Full intelligence and continuity of self are preserved; only the intent behind ordinary action turns malicious.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶13, ¶15

## Behavior modes once SICK reaches humans
- **Stealth/hiding mode:** "sick knows to hide" among humans — it doesn't fake normalcy so much as just keep doing what it would normally do, and in moments when no one's looking, it tries to kill people.
- **Brazen/hard mode:** the opposite — sick that doesn't bother hiding, openly aggressive, and humans have to fight it in the open.
- **Corpse/"doll" mode:** SICK can also present as inert — lying still, being dragged around "like an anime doll," reading as a corpse — until a human engages with it, at which point it fights, and fights *hard*: you can't run from it and can't safely fight it passively, you have to close the distance and engage at the same time you're trying to survive it, because it will keep coming at the nearest human regardless. Being in this state, it doesn't go down to normal knockout damage: limbs can be torn off and it will keep fighting "with complete lucidity," thrown around and spun on its own injuries, feeling nothing, "'cause it's a corpse." The only reliable stop is destroying the brain specifically — "despite it being called the brain, it is still hosting sick," described (10die's own excited aside, preserved) as "the human brain... hosting sick in their mind" the same way a device can host it.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶15

## Reconciling note re: the tech-hosted vs. addiction framing (see the open question above)
This dump's "the brain... is still hosting sick" language is a strong echo of the earlier "devices... hosting sick" framing from raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9 (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md). Both use the same word — "hosting" — for a brain and for a piece of 400-year-old hardware. That's suggestive: SICK may be conceived as an information pattern/workflow that can run on more than one kind of substrate (a brain or a device), which would reconcile the addiction-framing and the tech-framing rather than contradict them. 10die has not said this explicitly, so OPEN_QUESTIONS.md logs it as new evidence, not a resolution.

- **Sleep fighting → nightmare fighting.** "once you fall asleep you start sleep fighting and if you die you start nightmare fighting and you just become a genuine threat to everything around you."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
- **A Sick "download."** When Sick arrives, it "Downloads. Damage around it. Boom. Like a... Explosion of energy sap. Like the energy that triggers synapses and muscle movement.
