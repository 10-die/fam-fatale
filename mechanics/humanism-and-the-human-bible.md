# Humanism and the human Bible

New in 2026-10-07. Contradictions and open questions are logged in canon/_oq/humans.md.

Not a source: 10die also has a repo called `rad-human-bible`. It's a game prototype, not lore. Nothing in this file comes from it.

## What it is
- "In the story, I wanted humans to have a human Bible. And what happens is humans started, humans created a new religion called humanism."
- **The practice:** "humans would write down what happens in their lives and what they think about it and how they feel. And then in a book. And then they were able to access other people's books."
- **The effect:** "And humanity started being influenced by one another directly. And we'd start reading stories of other humans who are struggling when they were struggling."
- **Why it unites:** "it creates a unity between humans because it bypasses all other metrics and just says, are you human? Can I sit next to you and be at peace?"
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 1

## Rad and the Bible
- "And the conflict with Rad is they don't have Bibles. They, they would have to start."
- "Maybe Keegan starts with a Bible. That's how the Rad start join the, the, the Gucci start joining Keegan. Uh, the humans." From there 10die moves straight into the new border nation built by Deguchi rad and humans together (see forces/humans.md, *A new nation on the border*).
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 1
- Later in the same session: "maybe this would be the first thing that Six starts to write in their Bible when they start interacting with humans. And the humans say, you can do a Bible, your own Bible, your human Bible." "This" refers to Keegan's habit of fighting sick rad while sober, "As a test for itself, seeing what it can and can't get away with."
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3
- "Six" (and "Sig" in the same message) seems to mean Keegan, but that's a transcription guess, not confirmed. See canon/_oq/humans.md. Keegan as a character belongs to characters/keegan-deykwein.md.
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3
- Note the wording: the humans offer a rad "your own Bible, your human Bible." So a rad's Bible is still called a "human Bible." Whether that's deliberate (humanism as something a rad can join) isn't stated.
  source: raw/2026-10-07-story-world-building-notes.md, msg 3

## How it sits next to other canon (not linked by 10die)
- Humanism's test ("are you human? Can I sit next to you and be at peace?") stands against the 2026-10-10 line that humans "do not trust Rad one iota." 10die doesn't put them side by side; see canon/_oq/humans.md.
- Streaming is everywhere in human society (King's livestream, people's own streams, children's co-streams; see forces/human-society.md). 10die never connects the human Bible to streaming. Any link is a question, not canon.
