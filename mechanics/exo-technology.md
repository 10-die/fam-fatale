# EXO Technology

## Core concept
EXO are removable exoskeletons that supplement and supercharge existing fighter abilities. They snap onto fighters and amplify what they already do well, rather than replacing or introducing entirely new capabilities.

## Implementation examples
- **Mary Sue's EXO:** Amplifies her existing kicks to become "ridiculous" — taking her natural parkour/mobility-based striking and enhancing the power/impact to a superhuman level.
- **Loading's EXO:** Amplifies Loading's arm/punch mechanics. Enables "rocket punches" — Loading's already-powerful arm strikes are converted into or combined with propulsive force, effectively turning punching into a ranged/explosive attack.

## Integration philosophy
EXO technology is described as "tools supplementing what they already do well" — not arbitrary power-ups but tactical enhancements that extend natural fighting styles rather than contradicting them. A fighter's core strategy doesn't change; the scale and lethality of their existing techniques increase.
