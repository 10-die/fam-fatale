# RAD family fight-radiation link

## What creates the link
- RAD who fight alongside each other and "spread their fight damage radiation" with one another become physically linked — this is explicitly why Keegan is *not* linked to his own family (he hasn't been fighting with them) but *is* linked to Mary Sue (he has been fighting with her). The link tracks who you actually fight beside, not blood relation by itself — though for Kyle's family it does track along blood/household lines because they fight together as a unit.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2

## The orbit effect
- Demonstrated in the Kyle/Mary Sue hallway scene (see characters/kyle.md). If one linked RAD in a group tries to physically pull away while the others keep moving, the others get physically dragged toward/after them — feet sliding on the floor — rather than being able to simply walk off and leave the linked member behind.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2

## What it does in a fight — the door-shove metaphor
10die's own illustration, worth preserving close to verbatim per the harness doc's "don't clean up the voice" rule:

> "Two guys walk up to a door. One... they're prepared to shove their shoulder into the door to open it with force, while the other one lurches back into the wall and pushes off of force. The door swings open, and the guy in the back slams into the guy in front and pushes him with all his force, and he has just shaped his body into a kick position. And he gets thrown into someone with a slicing kick, a powerful kick. If you were half, bro, you get banged straight."

- Reading: linked RAD can use each other kinetically — one plants and shoves, using their partner's own momentum/positioning to launch them into an attack with more force than either could generate alone.
- The danger of fighting a linked pair: they will use one another, throw one another, and deliberately position themselves between you and their partner to dodge your hits while still landing their own.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶2

## When linked fighters lose
- "And when linked and lose, you're knocked out and disoriented when you wake." (Said in the McConnell passage; see forces/rad-territories.md.)
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-4.md, ¶18

- When Keegan beats a rad back to life, "if you punch Keegan while Keegan is hitting its family member, its ally, I guess its family member, it bonds with whoever it's healing. That's how the rad work."
