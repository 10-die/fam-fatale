# Rad movement — friction, momentum, the drop strike

How rad (and anyone using radioactive blood) move in a fight. Combat damage rules are in mechanics/damage-and-combat.md. Individual fighting styles (Keegan's start-stop and moon jumps, Mary Sue's dodging, Loading's finger gun and wings) belong to their character files under characters/.

## Friction as a variable
- Who can do it: "this is something that's accessible to those who use radioactive blood because the way they interact with physics on a mathematical level on a variable based level can be altered and warped."
- What it looks like: Sick, after throwing a corpse, flies back into the building "maneuvering doing damage kicking the walls and essentially slide glide tap dance skating across the surface and being able to change direction on a dime."
- How it works: "so they'll start skipping and then the friction between their ground, the ground and their feet when they skip can sometimes decrease or increase so they glide, glide, moving quickly no friction then high friction, a turn immediately keeping momentum unless they strike and they strike with that momentum."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

## Momentum is kept until the strike
- From the same passage: momentum carries through a high-friction snap turn ("a turn immediately keeping momentum") and is spent only on a hit ("unless they strike and they strike with that momentum").
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1
- Recoil as movement: in the balcony scene Sick "throws the corpse so hard that it itself flies back into the building through the building."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

## The drop strike
- "I imagine the moment of Sick dropping from a position that's tall and aiming to land right near something that's alive and then as it lands it bends its legs and strikes down and that damage is transferred into the ground at that point of impact with the fist."
- Area damage: "So anything close enough will take the damage of a fall from that height or a bit less or a bit less a range of just lethal damage in any case."
- Small version: "but it can be done even with a jump jumping near someone and then smashing the ground."
- Weakness: "you're vulnerable while you're going down if someone hits you they cancel that, that what you're doing and have an advantage."
- This is followed directly by the principle that fights should last several moments so everyone gets a chance to try (mechanics/damage-and-combat.md).
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1

## Sick's close-quarters movement (the "sick in the room" scene)
- In a room with a three-meter ceiling, Sick drops "underneath the kick, doesn't just dodge it, drops to the floor" and punches upward; slides "towards the human it punched on the ground still and then standing upright suddenly"; is kicked into a wall, "had already turned around after being kicked. And kicks itself off the wall back where it came from."
  source: raw/2026-10-04-mary-sue-collective-governance.md, msg 2
