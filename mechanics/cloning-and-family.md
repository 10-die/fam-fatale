# Human cloning & "family"

## The system
- The human government runs a cloning program for humans killed or lost in RAD territory: it uses whatever DNA it can recover (sperm, egg, or the body itself) to rebuild the person. Growth is accelerated/"altered" — roughly a week to reach adulthood.
- Clones don't retain memories. "We don't get to keep our memories. Our bodies are just rebuilt by the government."
- The government has deliberately decided *not* to allow multiple living clones of the same person at once — only one version of you is alive at a time.
- Because of that rule, it's your family's responsibility to report your death — the system doesn't necessarily know on its own. And there's a grim window this implies: "even though you are dead, your... clone[']s corpse may be fighting out there" — i.e., a body belonging to you can still be out there fighting after you've died, before that death is reported/processed.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11, ¶13
- Tracking is depicted in explicitly corporate/tech terms: a "CTO" talking to "a team of developers," tasked with locating specific missing humans, visualized as "a map of America that is blank except for three dots."
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶13

## "Family" as a social unit, not a blood unit
- Humans "group up in families" that are explicitly **not** blood family — they're "the family you respawned with." 10die's own illustration: someone dies, their (blood) family dies too/is gone, and they respawn among other people — that becomes their family instead.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11
- This is presented as deliberate tonal counterweight to how dark/dreary the setting could otherwise read — see canon/tone-and-theme.md. It's explicitly the context 10die places the Karen/Mary Sue "beating heart" relationship inside.
  source: raw/2026-07-20-brainstorm-session-2.md, ¶11

## Note on RAD family-links vs. human respawn-families
Not stated to be the same mechanism. RAD family bonds are earned through fighting alongside each other (see mechanics/family-link.md) and are physical/kinetic. Human "families" here are about who you end up surviving/respawning among after death. Worth keeping distinct rather than conflating — 10die introduces them in completely separate contexts.
