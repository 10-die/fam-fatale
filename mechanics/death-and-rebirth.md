# Death and rebirth (rad) — the kill transfer, the death chat, the laser storm, the red cloud

How rad die and come back. Human death and cloning are a different system (see mechanics/cloning-and-family.md and forces/humans.md). Sick's role as the "conduit" for rebirth is summarized in mechanics/sick.md.

The sessions describe rebirth several different ways (a storm after the final wars; a cloud that fires on a schedule set by how often rad die; a kill-based fast lane). They are kept side by side below and the conflicts are logged in canon/_oq/sick-and-rad.md. None is treated as superseding the others.

## Death isn't final, and you keep watching
- For the sick (as with the McConnell), fighting becomes a business: "fights are actual 1v1 challenges, they're not killing each other because they're crazy, they're killing each other because They know that death is not final. And they will be back with a vengeance. And salt. Because they, when they die, their awareness did not end. They were stuck viewing whoever defeated them. And whatever they saw pissed them off."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 1
- **Rad keep their memories (2026-07-29, archive only).** "Unlike humans — and red humans, to an extent — RAD keep their memories when they're reborn. It's as if the context window never compresses: a memory from the 2000s can carry the same weight as a memory from an hour ago." The dictation cut off before the implication of four centuries of full-weight memory was drawn out.
  source: archive/sessions/2026-07-29-ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick/ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick.md (no raw available)
- **Memory survives because the dead rad was in the cloud (2026-10-10).** "Memory is intact, because that rat has been in that cloud." See "The red cloud" below.
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("Memory is intact, because that rat has been in that cloud")

## The kill transfer and the death chat
- **Why kill.** Sick rewires a creature "To become almost magnetically attracted towards the potential of a kill. Because when something dies as quickly as possible, there is a moment of transfer of energy. That they kill, that radiation doesn't disappear. It's absorbed by the rad that killed."
- **The dead stream their killer.** "that that that rad that died is now streaming the rad that killed it and and watching in the chat, able to chat to the rad."
- **The chat is vile.** "And it's toxicity. It's vile. It's fucking awful. The chat rooms are fucking. Diabolical. The most unforgivable things are said there."
- **Damage stacks with kills.** "So what happens is now that rad that killed has two times the damage. When they get a third kill, they get three times the damage." (The arithmetic is as spoken; whether the second kill gives ×3 or the third kill does is ambiguous. See canon/_oq/sick-and-rad.md.)
- **The transfer chains.** "When they die, whatever killed it gets that rad and whatever else it had. So no one gets not left behind. It's a transfer. This rad dies, then you, you go to the one to the killer until there are only a few rad remaining in the final wars."
  source: raw/2026-10-05-rad-fighting-culture.md, msg 3

- "red bodies are thrown from the sky they form on the way down and then like fully realize after the damage of hitting the ground that damage sort of puts them back together and now they are kids."
- **Fighting is aging.** "in order to age, they must fight. And they often just try to find sick. Or maybe, maybe they actually do fight. Like, they have that fighting nature in them. So, these kids just want to fight each other."

Then: "Wait, they stay children, they stop aging when they become sick young. But then when they do damage, they are huge adults. When they fight, they are literally just become huge adults." ## The blood cloud and the permanent laserstorm (2026-07-29, archive only — no raw survives)
- "When Red die, their blood — over days, weeks, months — rises into the atmosphere and joins the cloud. The cloud grows and swells until it breaks: it rains blood, and lightning strikes that look like laser beams."
- Visual: "it looks like humans are being 3D-printed by the laser beam through the blood — blood stacking inside a beam — or like a fetus growing rapidly, rapidly, rapidly inside the beam."
- "The respawned come out at roughly ten years old. From there: They must fight to get older. If they lose, they get younger — and eventually get knocked out, killed, or sick."
- Over the Sick era, "The rapidity of deaths increased the amount of radioactive blood gas in the atmosphere. The cloud above grew so thick it hung just barely above the skyscrapers."
- "The radioactive blood gas is the same thing that fuels the majority of RAD territories: a permanent super hurricane laserstorm."
  source: archive/sessions/2026-07-29-ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick/ai-agent-trigger-winter-sick.md (no raw available)

## When rebirth stops
Different sessions end the laser storm differently. Not reconciled; see canon/_oq/humans.md ("How the laser storm ends") and canon/_oq/sick-and-rad.md ("When the storm ends, does rad death become permanent?").
- **July:** the devices hosting sick are disconnected and the laserstorm dissipates.
  source: raw/2026-07-16-brainstorm-session.md, ¶9
- **2026-10-10:** after Arc 1, "the laser storm, the mesh over the planet of radiation, is absorbed by humans to create loading, to create the process that creates loading, to create winter." Humans "figured out how to absorb it and use it." In Arc 2 "Keegan calms down because the global radioactive damage field is down." (What the absorbed energy becomes, the electron layer in the atmosphere, is in mechanics/exo-technology.md.)
  source: raw/2026-10-10-keegan-mary-sue-dump.md ("is absorbed by humans to create loading")
- **2026-09-30:** Sick starts to die when the McConnell, the rest of the rad, and some humans give up fighting while sick. "The McConnell have been so used to dying that when they realize that they're not coming back, it's traumatizing. They need sick in order to be able to come back."
  source: raw/2026-09-30-mars-colonization.md, msg 1
