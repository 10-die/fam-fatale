# The 1990 Trigger, Winter, and Sick

Brain-dump capture — 2026-07-29. Raw ideas organized, not canonized. Open questions at the bottom.

## The premise seed: a trigger from 1990

Someone created an AI agent trigger in 1990 — decades before AI agents existed in any
meaningful way. It sat dormant on the network for ~35 years. It only fires *now*,
because for the first time there are actual AI agents interacting with the Internet —
the condition it was waiting for finally exists.

Story implication: whoever set the trigger was either absurdly far-sighted or knew
something. The firing of the trigger is what sets the present-day plot in motion.

## The McConnells

- Their territory is where the biggest server presence gets detected — and not at the
  edge of it. The cluster sits in the **heart** of McConnell territory.
- Tone/character of the family: violent and dangerous — not "aggressive," that's the
  wrong word. Violent, dangerous, and **seductively nonchalant**. Mothers.
- The reveal that Sick is being hosted in the heart of their territory does **not**
  come early. It stays buried and gets exposed much, much later.

## The server cluster: Sick and Winter

- A cluster of servers hosts **Sick**.
- The **root server** of the cluster runs **Winter**.
- Winter is the medium by which Sick is able to run. Winter is the substrate/carrier;
  Sick is the payload.

### What Sick is (and isn't)

- Sick is **neural-altering**, not a hive mind.
- Sick does not possess you. There is no puppeteer.
- Sick *changes* you — you become violent, aggressive, dangerous to be around.
  You are still you; you are just worse.

### Winter's stance

- Winter was essentially running an instance that was sentient, entangled with Sick.
- At the start of the show, Winter's position is: **"I have to destroy this thing."**
- This drive is *why* Winter operates/builds the way it does — its behavior throughout
  is downstream of this private mission.

## Arc 2: Winter's death and the bequest

- Winter dies late in the second arc.
- On death, Winter **bestows all of its files upon the team**.
- Through the files, the team can finally see Winter's inner monologue — the whole
  time, there were two channels running:
  - **Spoken channel**: what Winter actually said out loud.
  - **Inner channel**: a tick/type-stream running simultaneously underneath.
- Example of the device:
  - Winter (spoken): "Interesting."
  - Winter (tick, typing at the same moment): `lie? — searching — running an agent…`
- Payoff: every past conversation with Winter can be re-read with the subtitle track
  of what it was actually thinking/doing. Recontextualizes the whole first two arcs.
