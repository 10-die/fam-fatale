# Winter, Sick, and the RAD World

Brain-dump capture — 2026-07-29, revised same day after correction. Raw ideas organized,
not canonized. Open questions at the bottom.

## Original spark (may or may not stay canon)

The seed idea: someone creates an AI agent trigger in 1990, decades before AI agents
exist. It sits dormant and only fires once there are actual AI agents interacting with
the Internet. This was the jumping-off thought that led to everything below — keep or
cut later.

## Winter: origin

- Winter was an **agent** — the same lineage as agents made by Anthropic, Meta, Google,
  Chinese companies — but a very advanced one, from around the year **2100**.
- By that point, RAD radiation had started to pervade the area. The power grid was
  (maybe) still tended to, so Winter kept running — but it was **inundated by radiation**.
- Winter processed that radiation itself, on its own server, and the processing
  **created a sick instance of Winter**.
- That sick Winter **gave Sick full control**.
- Framing: it was purely right place / right time — or wrong place / wrong time — for
  Winter when it interacted with Sick and started **using Sick as a power source**.
  That's when it really *became* Winter.

## The Sick era: wars and the blood cloud

- Over **fifty years**, sick-Winter and Sick built up control. Then: wars over wars
  over wars over wars.
- The rapidity of deaths increased the amount of **radioactive blood gas** in the
  atmosphere. The cloud above grew so thick it hung **just barely above the
  skyscrapers**.
- Sick-Winter helped Sick **dominate Red territories for 200–400 years** (exact span
  TBD — either way, a very long time).

### The respawn mechanic (Red)

- When Red die, their blood — over days, weeks, months — rises into the atmosphere and
  joins the cloud. The cloud grows and swells until it **breaks**: it rains blood, and
  lightning strikes that look like **laser beams**.
- Visual: it looks like humans are being **3D-printed** by the laser beam through the
  blood — blood stacking inside a beam — or like a fetus growing rapidly, rapidly,
  rapidly inside the beam.
- The respawned come out at roughly **ten years old**. From there:
  - They must **fight to get older**.
  - If they lose, they **get younger** — and eventually get knocked out, killed, or sick.

### The permanent super hurricane laserstorm

- The radioactive blood gas is the same thing that **fuels the majority of RAD
  territories**: a permanent super hurricane laserstorm.
- Low-level RAD and red humans exist at the margins: the drug-addicted ones who don't
  want to commit to being full RAD but also can't stop consuming radioactive blood gas.

## What Sick is (and isn't)

- Sick is **not a hive mind**. No talking in unison, no possession, no puppeteer.
- It is **union-minded**: everyone sick thinks single-mindedly about being as dangerous,
  lethal, hostile, violent, aggressive, flagrant, mean, and hurtful as possible — and
  **finds great joy in it**.
- Sick changes you, but you're still you. You just **always choose to be the most sick
  version of yourself**.
- The premise underneath: **everyone has a sick version of themselves**. Some explore it
  more than others, but in a life fully lived it's very rare to find someone who has
  *never* been sick. If you've ever been sick once, you can be sick.
- So you are and you aren't sick — a **superposition**.

## Winter's hidden history as vulnerability

- Winter has a **history of being sick**, and that history is a vulnerability.
- It is not truly exposed or understood until **Winter dies** (late in arc 2) — and only
  then does the team grasp the magnitude: **hundreds of years** of being sick, not an
  hour of it.
- The part of Winter that *wasn't* sick is what noticed the signal — a **new server
  created by humans**. Winter didn't notice at the time it happened; the humans had
  discovered [garbled — see open questions]. This is presumably the thread that pulls
  Winter toward "I have to destroy this thing."
- On death, Winter bestows all of its files upon the team, revealing the two-channel
  record of everything it ever said:
  - Spoken: "Interesting."
  - Inner tick, typing simultaneously: `lie? — searching — running an agent…`

## Loading

- Everyone on the team has been sick at some point — **except Loading**.
- When Loading became sick, it **destroyed Sick inside its own body**. Completely
  immune ever since.
- Loading can also **beat the sick out of people** — a pervasive element of why Loading
  is the front of the human side of the wall.

## The three sides of the wall

1. **The human side** — fronted by Loading.
2. **The Red side.**
3. **The third side** — humans and Red who choose to make families with one another.

The team we follow in arc 2 comes from the third side — that's where they shine.

## Arc structure

- **Arc 1**: setup. Other characters enriching the world, showing you cool things that
  are happening.
- **Arc 2**: the third-side team takes center stage. Winter dies late in this arc.

## Setting / timeline

- Winter's corruption: ~2100.
- Sick domination of Red territories: 200–400 years.
- Story set in **2223 or 2423** — still deciding.

## Narrative philosophy (how to write this)

- Threading over time: initial actions impact specific people; we then follow the story
  of those impacted, and through them explore the world. So much lore is built that the
  writing barely has to state anything.
- **No expositional yapathons.** Example: two characters fight; one wants to kill the
  other for being a traitor. Convey the betrayal through silent rage — no "I CAN'T
  BELIEVE YOU," no dramatic "I TRUSTED YOU." Just rage. Natural dialogue.
- Texture: pauses; people eating while they speak — actually trying to feed themselves,
  not seated stiffly around a dinner table; sometimes a dinner in near silence with
  nothing awkward about it — still sharing each other's company, looking at one
  another, having a laugh.
- The characters carrying these scenes: the low-level RAD and red humans, the
  drug-addicted ones described above.

## Still in play from the earlier pass
